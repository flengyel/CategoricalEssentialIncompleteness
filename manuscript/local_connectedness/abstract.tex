\begin{abstract}
We prove a categorical criterion for essential incompleteness,
developing the algebraic approach to incompleteness proposed by
Di Paola and Heller. The criterion ensures that every consistent
extension represented by a restriction idempotent has inseparable
theorem and refutation domains. The proof uses Turing morphisms and
range functors into locally connected restriction categories.
Its numerical application recovers essential incompleteness of
Peano arithmetic. From Miller's sequence we construct a consistent
theory of real closed fields whose consistent extensions are incomplete
whenever their axiom sets are semidecidable uniformly from all binary
representations of the sequence. We also construct a recursion category
from cosheaves on a three-point space. Its restriction splitting contains
a consistent propositional theory whose consistent extensions
represented by restriction idempotents are all incomplete.
\end{abstract}
