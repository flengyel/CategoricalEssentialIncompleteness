\begin{abstract}
We prove essential incompleteness theorems using the categorical
recursion theory initiated by Di Paola and Heller. We construct
consistent theories whose theorem and refutation domains remain
inseparable under every consistent extension represented by a
restriction idempotent. In the classical recursion category, we recover
essential incompleteness of Peano arithmetic.
From Miller's sequence we construct a consistent
theory of real closed fields whose consistent extensions are incomplete
whenever their axiom sets are semidecidable uniformly from all binary
representations of the sequence. We also construct a recursion category
from cosheaves on a three-point space. The category obtained by splitting
its restriction idempotents contains a consistent propositional theory
whose consistent extensions represented by restriction idempotents
are all incomplete.
\end{abstract}
