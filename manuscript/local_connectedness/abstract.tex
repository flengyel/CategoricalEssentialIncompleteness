\begin{abstract}
We construct internal deductive systems for propositional and first-order
logic within completions of recursion categories, addressing
Di Paola and Heller's objective to formulate generalized incompleteness
in categorical algebra. First-order logic with equality is realized in completions
of syntactic recursion categories and of a recursion category constructed
from a Miller sequence. Propositional logic is realized in the completion
of a recursion category constructed from cosheaves on a three-point space.
These constructions take place in the regular categories of total
morphisms in the restriction splittings.
Initial algebras describe syntax and finite derivations, and regular
images of conclusion morphisms give the generated theories.
A range functor interprets the theorem and refutation domains in a second
category. A soundness factorization through a validity subobject disjoint
from its inverse image under negation makes these interpretations disjoint.
A total reduction and a lift of a partial conditional then allow the comparison theorem to prove
incompleteness in the source. Total reductions from inseparable pairs
make incompleteness persist under consistent represented extensions.
In arithmetic, we recover recursive inseparability of theorem and
refutation sets for every consistent computably enumerable extension of
Peano arithmetic, with an independent sentence constructed uniformly
from an index for its axiom enumeration. We prove soundness by
interpreting syntax codes in standard arithmetic and formulas in a model
of the represented axioms, and transport the resulting soundness factorization to arithmetic
ultrapowers. The Miller construction gives a consistent theory of real
closed fields with constants naming the sequence. Every consistent extension whose axiom set is
semidecidable by one oracle program from every binary representation of
the sequence is incomplete. The cosheaf construction gives two
propositional theories whose consistent computably enumerable extensions
in the same language are incomplete.
\end{abstract}
