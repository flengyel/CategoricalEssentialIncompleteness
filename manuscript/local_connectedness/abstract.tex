\begin{abstract}
We prove essential incompleteness theorems using the categorical
recursion theory initiated by Di Paola and Heller.
In the classical recursion category, we recover
essential incompleteness of Peano arithmetic.
From Miller's sequence we construct a consistent
theory of real closed fields whose consistent extensions are incomplete
whenever their axiom sets are semidecidable uniformly from all binary
representations of the sequence. We also construct a recursion category
from cosheaves on a three-point space. Component evaluation of formula
and derivation objects in its Karoubi envelope gives a family of
consistent propositional theories. These theories admit no compatible
family of complete consistent extensions with computably enumerable
theorem sets.
\end{abstract}
