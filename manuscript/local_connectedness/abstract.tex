\begin{abstract}
We derive essential incompleteness from categorical inseparability in the
recursion theory initiated by Di Paola and Heller. For the incompleteness
applications, we construct total morphisms to sentence objects and prove
that the inverse images of theorem and refutation domains contain
inseparable pairs. This argument recovers essential incompleteness of
Peano arithmetic. From Miller's sequence we construct a recursion category
and a consistent theory of real closed fields. Categorical inseparability
implies that every consistent extension whose axiom set is semidecidable
uniformly from all binary representations of the sequence is incomplete.
We also construct a recursion category $\mathscr R_X$ from cosheaves on a
three-point space $X$. Formula and derivation objects in the Karoubi
envelope of $\mathscr R_X$ give, by component evaluation, a compatible
family of consistent propositional theories. Categorical inseparability
excludes compatible families of complete consistent extensions of these
theories with computably enumerable theorem sets.
\end{abstract}
