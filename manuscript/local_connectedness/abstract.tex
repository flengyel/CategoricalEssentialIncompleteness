\begin{abstract}
We derive essential incompleteness from a categorical construction of
inseparable pairs. The construction uses a comparison theorem for range functors
and specified lifts of partial morphisms into binary coproducts. We recover
recursive inseparability of the theorem and refutation sets of every
consistent computably enumerable extension of Peano arithmetic.
From Miller's sequence we construct a recursion category and a consistent
theory of real closed fields. Every consistent extension of this theory
whose axiom set is semidecidable uniformly from all binary representations
of the sequence is incomplete. The Miller example also gives a faithful
range functor preserving restriction products that sends an inseparable
pair to a separable pair. The same categorical method, applied to a
recursion category constructed from cosheaves on a three-point space,
gives essentially incomplete propositional theories.
\end{abstract}
