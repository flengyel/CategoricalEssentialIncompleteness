## Outline and dependency order

1. Define the internal theory, its global category, the represented internal
   hom, and two modal endofunctors on their different ambient categories.
   Obtain the introspective transformation from initiality.
2. Define the contradiction object. Apply Löb on the base to prove
   irrefutability, and the global-hom identity to characterize noninhabitation.
   Apply Löb separately on the global category for second incompleteness.
3. Construct coherent comparison maps from internal arithmetic functors.
   Obtain a class of theories and extensions from initial internal models.
4. Verify the canonical internal initial example and locate the remaining
   extension problem in the correct category.

## Results and scope

Theorem 2.1 gives an undecided subterminal in the fixed initial arithmetic
universe I for every consistent internal arithmetic universe C. It assumes
no reflection principle, universal coding object, inseparable pair, or
finite-proof encoding. Corollary 2.2 gives the separate second-incompleteness
assertion in Glob(C). Proposition 3.1 proves extension comparison and
coherence; the paragraph following it constructs extensions via initial models.

Undecidability in I is not yet undecidability in every Glob(C). Essential
incompleteness of all intended internal extensions remains unproved by this
route. The next attempt is an intrinsic proof/refutation comparison for
those extensions, retaining consistency alone rather than imposing soundness.
No novelty claim is made for the consequences of Ramesh's theorems.
