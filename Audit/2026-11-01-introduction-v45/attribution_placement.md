# Attribution at construction openings

The main missing opening attributions are in the syntactic-category and Miller sections. Several other sections already cite the relevant source when its construction or definition is introduced. The placement requirement is the author's instruction; it should not be turned into an assertion that every new result must have an external source.

Scope: all 14 compiled section source files and four appendices were checked for section/subsection openings and existing citation uses. This is an attribution-placement review, not a new proof audit or an independent re-verification of the entire bibliography. Proposed locators are already used in the paper. No manuscript files were edited.

## Openings already adequate

- Appendix A.2 names Goguen–Thatcher–Wagner–Wright and Goguen–Burstall in its first paragraph, then names Fiore–Plotkin–Turi, Gabbay–Pitts and Katsumata when binding/substitution precedents are distinguished. Keep these attributions and their differences; no extra list of authors is needed at the whole appendix opening.
- Appendix A.4 opens with Aczel's finite-proof characterization of finitary inductive closure, and states that the rule presentation is expressed in a slice category here. This correctly separates the precedent from the present categorical formulation.
- Appendix A.5 names Hájek–Pudlák at the propositional schemes and Hirst–Hirst at the quantifier/equality schemes. These are near the beginning and attached to the exact borrowed calculus. Appendix A.6 applies these previously cited schemes; an internal reference is adequate.
- Subsection 5.1 begins with the intended application in the total restriction splitting and credits Robinson–Rosolini in its first paragraph. Its later Cockett–Hofstra recognition citations are attached to the relevant theorem and should stay there.
- Section 9's frame definition already cites Heller exactly when the imported notion is used. A parenthetical source there is more accurate than claiming that Heller constructed this example.
- Appendix B continues the three-point construction defined and cited in Section 9. Its cosheaf equivalence, regularity computations, universal object, frame isomorphisms and Turing morphism are proved here. No outside author should be assigned those results without evidence of an actual borrowed construction.

## Sections that should not acquire artificial citations

Sections 3 and 4 present the comparison theorem and its application to the internally constructed theories. Section 7 applies these results to arithmetic; its indexed family, uniform independent sentences and numerical realization point to their internal prerequisites. Section 9's inseparability, component evaluation and propositional application likewise use proved constructions. Appendix C is this paper's lifting counterexample. Appendix D combines the interpretation functors already defined in Section 6.

These openings need internal cross-references and explicit objects/functors. They do not require an external attribution merely to make every section begin with a citation. In particular, Bernardi is a relevant antecedent for precomplete equivalence relations and the numeral-theory comparison, not the author of the present internal finite-derivation objects or comparison theorem. The existing Bernardi citation at the precise later comparison should not be moved to imply otherwise.
