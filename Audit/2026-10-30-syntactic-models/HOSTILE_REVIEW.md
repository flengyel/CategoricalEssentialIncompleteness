# (D) Cross-section consistency failures

None confirmed. The manuscript retains the following necessary distinctions.

1. For consistent c.e. T extending PA, the source S_T is not locally connected. Di Paola–Montagna Corollary 4.4 is explicitly attributed. The connected tests in the proof belong to the larger target and need not be source images. Faithfulness does not require their existence in the source.
2. The model-family target is a split restriction category of families of partial functions, not a recursion category. Its interpreted universal program cannot enumerate all partial functions at a model coordinate.
3. The target has Boolean domain lattices. Images of disjoint source pairs are therefore separable there. The lifting theorem asks for the particular sections attached to source complements, not lifts of arbitrary target separators.
4. The model functor is semantic and need not be effective. The effective observations of Section 5 are a separate operation on domains and numeral instances. They are not described as a functor from all of S_T to the classical recursion category.
5. Logical negation in the proof-observation argument is not confused with inverse image along a code constructor. No claimed theorem image in a completion is used.
6. The example T=Th(N) correctly shows why internal inseparability alone does not prove incompleteness. There the missing effective hypothesis is a c.e. axiom presentation.
7. LOC supplies the local-connectedness construction and lifting theorem; Montagna and Di Paola–Montagna supply the unprimed syntactic categories; Bernardi is identified as prior work for the alternative precomplete-equivalence route. The manuscript claims neither a new proof of these classical results nor completion of the full Di Paola–Heller program.

# (E) Acceptance conditions, non-negotiable

All conditions below are satisfied in the inspected revision. They are preservation conditions for subsequent edits, not requests for additional machinery.

1. **Uniform quotient and whole-model semantics.** Deliverable: equations `eq:uniform`–`eq:model-functor`. Endpoint: composition and range witnesses range over the entire interpreted type, and equality remains one uniform proof. Replacing either condition would invalidate the functor proof.
2. **Exact lifting obligation.** Deliverable: Proposition `prop:local-switch`, Theorem `thm:local-lifting`, and Lemma `lem:switch`. Endpoint: the target section is total, a source preimage is supplied, and totality of a general source lift is not inferred. Failure would require withdrawing the local-connectedness application.
3. **Source/target separation.** Deliverable: the paragraph following Theorem `thm:faithful` and the discussion after Corollary `cor:pair`. Endpoint: no local connectedness of S_T or preservation of inseparability into the Boolean target is asserted. Reversing these conclusions would be false.
4. **Effective bridge.** Deliverable: Section 5. Endpoint: c.e. proof observation and the standard recursive inseparable pair remain explicit. Removing them would leave the essential-incompleteness conclusion unsupported by the model functor alone.
5. **Historical scope.** Deliverable: the citations and concluding comparisons. Endpoint: any future novelty or strict-generalization claim must have a separate typed comparison and primary-source check. Such a claim is not approved by this report.
