## Discharged and retained assumptions

For the three-point category the effective observation functor, its
arrow action and composition law, endpoint Turing universality, domain
observations, and compatibility with computational extensions are
constructed. The existing syntax/proof-algebra and consistency theorem is
used with its already verified hypotheses.

No decidable equality on the universal cosheaf object is needed.
No global numerical coding of pushout quotients is introduced.
Syntax components need only be c.e.: the final observed pairs are on N²,
obtained by total inverse image along the atom map.

Extensions remain rule-closed computational domains in the fixed
partial-map category. A new syntactic category for every external
theory, or an observation functor for every recursion category, is not
constructed. The next problem is to characterize effective probes or
observation functors that preserve the specified inseparable pair and
the atomic proof inclusions, including compatible changes of presentation.

The conference history is not used as mathematical evidence or as a
priority argument. The paper identifies its application of the LOC
theorem and credits the established arithmetic theorem and prior
inseparability methods.
