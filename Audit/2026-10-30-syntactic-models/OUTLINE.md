# Actual outline and hypothesis accounting

1. **The syntactic recursion category:** formal types, uniform proof
   equality, PA-verified graph operations, restriction domains and ranges,
   and the Turing morphism.
2. **Interpretation in models:** object and arrow actions of the covariant
   F_M:S_T→Pfn; proof of structure preservation.
3. **A faithful family and theory extension:** all arithmetic structures on
   carrier N satisfying T; product target; faithfulness by completeness
   and countable Löwenheim–Skolem; local connectedness and weak choice;
   commuting extension square. Explicit contrast with DPM Corollary 4.4.
4. **Switching and the local-connectedness theorem:**
   - Connected-domain alternative.
   - Partial monicity, splitting of composite restrictions, and weak choice.
   - General range-functor lifting theorem, allowing a partial source lift.
   - One uniform dovetail program supplies every model-coordinate switch.
   - Inseparability in each S_T and preservation of the specified pair by
     theory-extension quotients.
5. **Effective observations and essential incompleteness:** numeral-instance
   observations; their faithful-representation equation; the covariant
   pair-valued extension functor in classical Rec; proof-search consequence.
6. **Three-point alternative:** finite connected subdiagrams and the full
   inclusion into the split ambient category.

Discharged for S_T: existence of a range functor into a split locally
connected weak-choice target, nonvanishing of the numerical identity,
preservation of the required operations, and the precise switch lifting.
The faithful family also supplies compatibility with extensions.

Retained hypotheses: consistent classical T⊇PA and the standard formalized
program-graph operations; c.e. axioms only for the effective observation
and logical incompleteness consequence.

Not inferred: local connectedness of S_T, target Turing universality,
effectiveness of the model-family functor, or lifting of arbitrary target
domains and separators. The original conditional construction notes remain
unchanged. No claim of historical novelty or completion of every
Di Paola–Heller formulation is made.
