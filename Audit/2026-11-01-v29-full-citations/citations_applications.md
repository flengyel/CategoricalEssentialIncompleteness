## CurryCosheaves

Primary text: <https://arxiv.org/pdf/1607.06942>. Location: Definition2.6, author PDFp3, with Definitions2.1–2.3.

Definition2.6 states the cosheaf axiom as preservation of the colimit indexed by the nerve of a cover. For Set this colimit is the displayed coequalizer of pairwise-intersection and cover-member coproducts: compatible maps on the members are exactly cocones on all their finite intersections. The empty cover requires the initial set. Thus the paper uses an equivalent explicit form, not a verbatim source formula. Publisher metadata verify the published2018 article; the primary author version verifies the locator and definition.

Uses: sections/09-three-point-space.tex:14.

## Keisler

Primary text: <https://people.math.wisc.edu/~hkeisler/ultraproducts-web-final.pdf>. Location: Theorem3.1 and Corollary3.2, author PDFp4.

Theorem3.1 is Los theorem for any first-order formula in an ultraproduct; Corollary3.2 expressly says that an ultraproduct of models of a theory models that theory. Apply to the constant family of standard arithmetic structures. The nonempty index set and proper ultrafilter are explicitly fixed in the paper.

Uses: sections/06-interpretation-in-models.tex:397.
