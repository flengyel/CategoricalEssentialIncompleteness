## Hypotheses and locators

The range citations do not establish regularity of the total category from split ranges alone. CGHRangeI gives an M-stable factorization system; CGHRangeII adds the finite-limit/stability/all-monics conditions. Appendix A independently assumes regular D and requires a partial inverse for its selected image monic in the alternate realization. Those qualifications are preserved.

One optional citation placement improvement: at Appendix A line649, move the reference to CGHRangeI Lemma4.12 to the earlier agreement-law definition. Theorem4.7 directly supplies the factorization claim; Lemma4.12 supplies the compatibility with the stronger range convention. Theorem4.5 and Section4.11 at line657 support the split-range construction and its agreement law respectively. This is not a false source attribution.

The list citations concern parameterized lists only. The paper separately assumes initial algebras for its other syntax functors; neither regularity nor an ordinary unparameterized natural-numbers object is used as a cited justification for those additional algebras.
