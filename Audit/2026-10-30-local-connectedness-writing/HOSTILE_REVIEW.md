# (D) Cross-section consistency failures

None confirmed. The following distinctions remain intact.

1. **Existence versus inseparability.** Heller and the corrected Lengyel-2004 theorem construct recursion categories from generation and iteration data. The present lifting theorem starts with a recursion category and uses a range functor. The new historical discussion does not interchange those hypotheses.
2. **Uniform versus pointwise provability.** The valid S_T quotient uses one uniform proof. The introduction excludes S' and S'_T under the proposed pointwise composition, under the consistency and c.e. hypotheses of the cited obstruction. It does not attribute that obstruction to the unprimed construction.
3. **Source versus target local connectedness.** Consistent c.e. S_T remains non-locally-connected, whereas the model-family target is locally connected. Faithfulness does not imply that its additional test domains lift to the source.
4. **Internal versus effective inseparability.** Internal inseparability in S_T is obtained from its model interpretation and switching lift. Arithmetic incompleteness additionally uses the numerical application of the same lifting theorem, finite PA-verifiable traces and effective proof enumeration. The complete theory Th(N) remains the explicit reason not to infer logical incompleteness from internal inseparability alone.
5. **The two three-point pairs.** The inclusion into the split ambient category verifies the local-connectedness theorem. The selected singleton-output pair used by the existing classical presentation has its endpoint inseparability checked by the explicit endpoint Turing formula. The manuscript does not identify it with the generally different summand-output pair of the displayed lifting theorem.
6. **Computational versus arbitrary domains.** The three-point category, its permitted partial maps and its computational extensions are unchanged. Ordinary subobjects of an enlarged category of all cosheaves are not silently added.
7. **Negation and inverse image.** The code constructor nu is total, while nu-star is monotone inverse image on domains. It records formulas whose negations belong to the domain and is not claimed to be Boolean complementation.
8. **Established scope.** No new construction hypothesis is discharged by this writing revision. The revised introduction does not claim a solution for every recursion category, a strict extension of Bernardi's theorem, or completion of all Di Paola–Heller formulations.
