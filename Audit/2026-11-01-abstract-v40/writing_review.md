## Contribution and relationships

The opening identifies internal deductive systems and their use in essential incompleteness. The syntactic realization then supplies a particular setting for first-order logic with equality. In its Peano-arithmetic instance, computably enumerable extensions are represented by internal theories in the completion. The next sentence applies a categorical comparison theorem to sound semantic interpretations and states incompleteness of both the internal theories and the corresponding arithmetic extensions, under consistency.

This sequence gives the comparison theorem an identifiable mathematical role. The arithmetic paragraph can no longer be read merely as an ordinary recursion-theoretic conclusion placed beside an unrelated categorical construction. Its general-to-specific progression also preserves the connection to Di Paola and Heller's objective.

The explicit internal conclusion matters: it makes the categorical contribution visible before giving its arithmetic consequence. The phrase concerning corresponding extensions explains the relation between that internal result and essential incompleteness of arithmetic. The sentence does not claim that soundness alone implies incompleteness; the named categorical comparison theorem supplies the intervening result, whose full hypotheses remain in the body.

## Selection of objects and detail

The abstract retains internal deductive systems, the two logics, recursion-category completions, the named syntactic categories, the represented arithmetic theories, their semantic interpretations, and the comparison theorem because their relationships state the principal construction and application. It omits axiom subobjects, conclusion images, total reductions, partial conditionals, and lift constructions because their definitions and proof roles belong in the introduction and body.

The Miller paragraph retains the sequence, expanded real-closed-field theory, and one-program/every-representation extension condition. These identify the application and its scope. The cosheaf paragraph retains the three-point space, propositional realization, two resulting theories, and consistent same-language computably enumerable extension class. It supplies a distinct application without suggesting that every application uses the arithmetic comparison argument.

Thus the selection is evaluated by the relations needed to convey each result, not merely by whether each noun can be removed in isolation.
