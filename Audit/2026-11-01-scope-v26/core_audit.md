## Main finding

The paper contains two routes to arithmetic incompleteness, as well as a uniform strengthening and a separate ultrapower extension. These are mathematically connected, but they are not one linear proof.

1. **The fixed syntactic category route** uses the diagonal comparison theorem (3.1), the logical comparison theorem (4.2), the internal realization in `B_PA = Tot(Split(S_PA))` (5.9), standard-code soundness (6.6), the arithmetic reduction (7.1), and a partial proof-search conditional (7.2). It yields the internal/standard parts of 7.3 and the recursively inseparable theorem/refutation pair in 7.4.
2. **The source-inseparability route** uses the locally connected section construction (3.3–3.5), an instance providing complementary lifts, and the source-domain criterion (4.3). Corollary 6.3, numerical §7.5, Miller, and the cosheaf application instantiate this route. The fixed syntactic comparison proof in §7.1 does not depend on Theorem 3.5.
3. **The indexed-family route** adds 7.5 to the fixed-category machinery and performs one computation with the presentation index as a parameter. It yields 7.6. The fact that each presentation separately has an independent sentence does not imply this uniform result.
4. **The ultrapower route** adds 6.7–6.9 to standard soundness and supplies the ultrapower clause of 7.3. Neither ordinary arithmetic incompleteness nor 7.6 requires it.

This separation is the main usable result of the audit. A substantial reduction requires choosing which representation and application branches belong in this paper. It cannot be obtained by treating the numerical initiality proof as a duplicate of the syntactic initiality proof.

## Apparent repetition that is not a valid deletion

**Syntactic versus numerical initiality.** Proposition 5.5 and Proposition 7.7 use closely related finite-tree algorithms, but assert universal properties in different categories. In `B_T`, targets and the equality of arrows are governed by T-provable functionality, totality, and graph equivalence. In `C_rec`, target operations need only be computable and total on their carriers; they may have no PA proof of totality. A denotation functor does not automatically preserve arbitrary initial algebras. Therefore one cannot infer numerical initiality merely by applying `G_N` to 5.5–5.8, nor infer syntactic initiality from extensional numerical folds. The current numerical proof reuses coding algorithms where possible but must address a different class of target algebras.

A shared abstract coding/fold lemma is conceivable, but it would need a precise ambient framework covering both proof-controlled and extensional realizations, with hypotheses strong enough to prove totality and uniqueness in each. That is a new theorem and proof obligation, not an available cut. It may not shorten the manuscript after its definitions are included.

**Standard versus ultrapower soundness.** Proposition 6.6 proves soundness on standard finite derivation codes. Theorem 6.9 uses a natural comparison to interpret the whole proof object in particular nonstandard models. The latter is additional mathematics; standard induction alone does not establish it. Move the entire extension if not needed, rather than replacing it by a sentence saying that soundness is unchanged.

**Single presentation versus indexed uniformity.** The proof-search argument in 7.2 and the parameterized argument in 7.6 have the same diagonal idea, but the latter requires a single represented computation for all presentations and the correct internal theory fibers. A choice of independent sentence for each theory does not produce the total arrow asserted by 7.6.

**Appendix construction versus main application.** Appendix A constructs syntax and least deductive subobjects under categorical hypotheses. Section 4 relates these subobjects to ranges and sound interpretation; Section 5 verifies the hypotheses in a particular total category. Those have different purposes. Basic splitting/range definitions recur in the standalone Appendix intentionally. Keeping Appendix A self-contained takes priority over eliminating every repeated definition.

**Complementary versus disjoint conditionals.** Lemma 6.2 constructs lifts from source complementarity proved in T. Lemma 7.2 searches between theorem/refutation domains that are only known to be disjoint after semantic soundness. They cannot be conflated without changing hypotheses.
