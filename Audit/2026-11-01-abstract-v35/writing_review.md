## Two local clarifications

1. The arithmetic sentence says “transport the factorization,” but no preceding sentence explicitly introduces a factorization. The regular-image construction earlier in the abstract also involves factorization, so the antecedent should be named. Replace this phrase by “transport the resulting soundness factorization.” This applies KLR §1, point 17, on unambiguous reading.
2. “A consistent theory of real closed fields” leaves the expanded language implicit. Section 8.3 defines the theory using constants for the Miller sequence together with its rational-cut axioms. Naming these constants in the abstract explains the sequence's logical role and avoids suggesting a statement about the complete theory in the usual ordered-field language. Use “a consistent theory of real closed fields with constants naming the sequence.” This is a local statement of the mathematical scope, not a new literature comparison.
