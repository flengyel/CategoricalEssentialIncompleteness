## (C) Minor issues

**C1 — Some comparison hypotheses are stronger than the diagonal calculation needs.** Anchor: Theorem 3.1, p. 5; `03-sections-and-inseparability.tex:19–22,46–78`. Claim as written: the source is a recursion category with ranges, and F is a range functor. Failure-mode label: `unused_hypothesis`, in the limited sense of an available weakening. Evidence: `derivation`; the vanishing calculation uses restriction products, the chosen b and Turing property, and preservation of composition, restrictions, zeros and coproducts. Preservation of ranges is not used in that calculation; the final assertion requires the stated join and its inverse-image preservation. The prodominical law and general isotypicality are not separately invoked after b is chosen. Downstream impact: none; the range framework is used in the construction and interpretation of theories. Severity: minor, optional statement economy. Repairability: the theorem could be weakened, but retaining a common range-functor setting is mathematically legitimate. This is not an acceptance condition.
