# (D) Cross-section consistency failures

None confirmed. The following distinctions are substantive and remain valid.

1. **The lifting hypothesis remains part of the theorem.** Proposition 6.4 supplies one sufficient condition. Appendix C explicitly concerns the weakened statement obtained by dropping lifting. Its example is not presented as a counterexample to the stated theorem.
2. **Source and target totality remain distinct.** The general lifting theorem permits a partial source lift; only its target image is known total. Neither the join criterion nor the oracle-domain example silently reverses preservation of totality.
3. **The computational category is retained.** All three-point syntax, proofs and domains lie in the explicit diagram or partial-map categories. The isotypical recursion category and the equivalence of its splitting with the ambient category are proved. Numerical endpoint observations do not replace the source by a numerical category.
4. **Internal consistency is the proved zero-domain equation.** The valuation argument is componentwise and finite. Because partial-map domains and inverse images are computed componentwise, it proves the stated equation in A. It does not assert an internal Boolean separator of the axiom domains or a natural family of valuations.
5. **Finite constructions are concrete here.** Appendix B uses actual finite formula/proof trees for this category. It does not import the private general iteration/worklist theorem, assume arbitrary countable arrow joins, or claim a general finite-stage union theorem for all recursion categories.
6. **Ranges are constructed directly.** The total-category image factorization, natural descent and pullback stability are proved. Partial ranges are their componentwise images. No general range completion, presheaf-image selection or preservation of a Turing morphism by a completion is assumed.
7. **The two numerical bridges retain their scope.** Arithmetic uses the numerical application of the lifting theorem plus PA-verifiable finite traces and c.e. proof enumeration. The three-point observation theorem uses its explicitly preserved endpoint Turing morphism and total atom map. Neither bridge claims that arbitrary range functors preserve arbitrary inseparable pairs.
8. **The three-point pairs are not silently identified.** The full inclusion verifies the general local-connectedness theorem. The singleton-output pair used for the constructed presentation has its endpoint inseparability verified directly. No unproved equality with the theorem's summand-output pair is needed.
9. **Computational extensions remain the stated class.** Their domains contain the base theorem domain, are closed under the logical rules, and satisfy the consistency equation. Completeness yields complementary c.e. preimages on N^2, where dovetailing is total. No arbitrary external subobjects or arbitrary external theories are added.
10. **No historical overclaim is introduced.** The original published construction and its corrections remain paired; the near-product clarification agrees with the source-check record. The invalid primed quotients remain distinguished from valid uniform S_T. The manuscript does not claim that the full Di Paola–Heller program or a strict extension of Bernardi's theorem has thereby been proved.

# (E) Acceptance conditions, non-negotiable

The conditions below are satisfied by the reviewed hash and must survive subsequent edits.

1. **Keep the actual structural constructions.** Deliverable: `prop:fork-total` and `prop:fork-structure`. Endpoint: regular-image factorization, natural descent, range agreement and the total-pullback image law remain proved. Removing them would reopen the range/regularity dependency.
2. **Keep exact domains in the Turing construction.** Deliverable: `thm:fork-turing`. Endpoint: component programs first test their specified domains, the central filter forms a subdiagram, and both directions of equality with each input partial arrow are proved. Failure would invalidate the recursion-category and endpoint applications.
3. **Keep finite syntax and proofs in the correct category.** Deliverable: `prop:fork-syntax-initial` and `thm:fork-proof-presentation`. Endpoint: initiality is in the total diagram category; proof carriers are c.e. with natural transitions; the conclusion range and its leastness are established. A change to arbitrary partial target algebras would require a different theorem.
4. **Retain the finite consistency argument.** Deliverable: the final part of `thm:fork-proof-presentation`. Endpoint: the valuation accounts for positive leaves of both proofs and uses disjointness for all negative leaves before the componentwise contradiction is converted to the domain equation. A global-separator assumption would not be an acceptable substitute.
5. **Preserve the conditional role of Appendix C.** Deliverable: `thm:local-lifting`, the Introduction and `prop:missing-lift`. Endpoint: the theorem keeps lifting and the example refutes only its omission, with separation understood in its specified domain order. An ordinary-recursive-separator claim would be false.
6. **Keep the effective observation hypotheses.** Deliverable: `cor:arithmetic` and `thm:cosheaf-observation`. Endpoint: c.e. proof enumeration or c.e. component observations and the total reductions remain explicit. Internal inseparability alone does not justify the incompleteness conclusions.
