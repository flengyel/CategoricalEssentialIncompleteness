## Initial review

The first draft said “construct independent sentences uniformly in axiom indices.” This was compressed and ambiguous: an axiom index can mean an index of an individual axiom, whereas Theorem 7.6 uses an index for a computably enumerable axiom enumeration. The sentence also left the theory relative to which independence holds implicit. The recommended repair identified the enumeration and bound the independent sentence to each consistent arithmetic extension.

The initial draft also said “whose axioms are semidecidable.” The recommended grammatical precision was “whose axiom set is semidecidable,” because membership in the set is semidecided.

No further changes were required in that draft within the reviewed scope. In particular, it placed the logical construction before the applications, distinguished the two semantic roles in the soundness statement, and preserved the Miller and propositional extension conditions.

## Final recheck

The saved abstract now says:

> For each such extension, an independent sentence is constructed uniformly from an index for its axiom enumeration.

The antecedent is the consistent computably enumerable extension of Peano arithmetic in the preceding sentence. The input to the uniform construction and the theory relative to which independence holds are therefore explicit.

The final abstract also names “categories of total morphisms in restriction splittings of recursion categories,” specifies that the reduction of the inseparable pair is total, and uses “axiom set” in the Miller sentence. These changes preserve the categorical and effectiveness distinctions without requiring formal declarations inside the abstract.
