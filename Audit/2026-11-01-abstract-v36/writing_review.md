Scope: organization of the revised opening only, using the existing Knuth–Larrabee–Roberts consultation. The unchanged remainder of the abstract was not audited again.

The opening proceeds from general to specific. Its first sentence states the construction of internal deductive systems for propositional and first-order logic in recursion-category completions and relates that construction to the Di Paola–Heller program. The next two sentences identify the first-order realizations in the syntactic and Miller completions and the propositional realization in the cosheaf completion. The following sentence identifies the common categorical setting.

The order makes the general construction precede its instances, preserves the distinction between the logical languages, and retains the precise role of restriction splitting and total morphisms. No organizational repair is needed within this narrow scope.
