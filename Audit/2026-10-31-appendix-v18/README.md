## Mathematical change

Lemma A.7/B.7 identifies the range of a total morphism with the partial identity on its regular image, assuming a split restriction category with ranges, a regular total category, and a partial inverse for the image inclusion. The proof establishes the least-receiver property directly. The partial-map instance cites the construction of split ranges from stable factorizations and the agreement condition for an epimorphic left class.

Sections 4, 8, and 9 now refer to B.7 at the range/image identification. The appendix also states how a restriction functor preserving ranges transports the splitting. The numerical realization proofs remain in the applications.
