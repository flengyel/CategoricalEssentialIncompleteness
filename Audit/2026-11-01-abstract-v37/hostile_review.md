## (D) Cross-section consistency failures

The wording “Di Paola and Heller's objective to formulate generalized incompleteness in categorical algebra” accurately describes their stated aim. On p. 594 they call for an algebraization of the generalized incompleteness theorem and describe the supporting category-theoretic development. They then enumerate formulations (1)–(5). On p. 595 they explicitly state that each implies incompleteness of every consistent recursively enumerable extension. Thus “generalized incompleteness” is their terminology, while essential incompleteness is an explicit consequence in their formulation. The two descriptions do not identify competing objectives.
