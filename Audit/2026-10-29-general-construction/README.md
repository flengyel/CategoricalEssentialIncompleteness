The substantive addition is a Turing construction of partial folds for typed
finitary inference polynomials. It discharges the separate partial-arrow gluing
assumption in the general finite-stage initial-algebra theorem. The specified
finite-stage domain unions remain existence hypotheses. The consistency and
essential-incompleteness results additionally require the stated regularity,
range/image agreement, decidable equality, and parameter-stability hypotheses.

The numerical split category realizes these hypotheses. Their derivation from
an appropriate general categorical iteration structure and comparison with
familiar arithmetic theories remain separate tasks. No completion of the
Di Paola–Heller program is claimed. No new Lean theorem is included.

The manuscript credits Heller (1990), §10, Example 5, p.1267, before discussing
the selected presheaf completion. The exploratory sheaf proposal in
`COMPLETION_RESEARCH.md` is not a theorem of the supplement.
