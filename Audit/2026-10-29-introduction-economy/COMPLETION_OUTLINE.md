## Governing dependency

The range-functor lifting theorem supplies an inseparable pair in a recursion category. A total map from that parameter object to the sentence object places the two domains in the inverse images of a theorem domain and its negation inverse image. Every consistent larger theorem domain has an inseparable theorem/refutation pair. Completeness would make that pair complementary, which is impossible.

For PA, the lifting theorem is instantiated at J:Rec→Pfn, and the proof-conclusion range and numeral substitution live in Split(Rec). The all-model functor F_T is a separate syntactic instance of the lifting theorem; its inseparability assertion alone cannot imply logical incompleteness, since it exists for complete non-c.e. theories.

For the three-point example, use the same coproduct-summand pair supplied by the lifting theorem for the positive and negative atomic axioms. Its endpoint pairs are numerical instances of the same theorem. This removes the change to a different singleton-output pair.
