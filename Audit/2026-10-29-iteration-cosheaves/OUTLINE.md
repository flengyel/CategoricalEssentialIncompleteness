# Outline and dependency order

## Mathematical question

Can Heller's finite-length iteration structure supply the union of
constructor stages, and can that construction support a nonnumerical
computational example without decidable atom equality?

## Order of construction

1. Define the partial category, its split domains and total category,
   universal ranges, weak choice, and strong agreement unions.
2. Give Heller's total semigroup functor and its typed structure maps,
   stable length coverage, isotypical objects, fixed frames, catalogues,
   and Turing developments. Distinguish the later iteration definition.
3. Prove the pullback law for full ranges. Use weak choice and kernel-pair
   descent to obtain regularity. State the factoriality alternative.
4. Recover finite domain joins from ranges of copairings; prove
   inverse-image, direct-image, coproduct, and finite-product union laws.
5. Construct finite execution traces from the total domain and value
   maps of a partial endomorphism. Descend endpoints along starts.
6. Define a typed finite-arity inference polynomial and a worklist
   recognizer. Compare height-bounded trees with size-bounded traversals.
   Use their cofinality to identify agreement unions, including parameters.
7. Invoke the unchanged supplement for the typed Turing fold and initiality.
8. Cover syntax and proofs by finite shapes. Use required atom equalities
   to prove consistency; no internal Boolean equality test is used.
9. Construct computational Sierpiński cosheaves, images, words, a universal
   object, its filtered evaluator, frames, and binary case distinction.
10. Prove nonnumericality and failure of decidable equality; apply the
    preceding construction. Explain the product obstruction for general
    cosheaves and state the next finite-space example.

## Definition dependencies checked

- Restriction domain → splitting → total category → ranges/weak choice/unions.
- Semigroup operations → finite-length coverage → trace object → iteration.
- Rule polynomial → constructor inverse → stages/worklist → tree carriers.
- Initial syntax → monic constructor tags → equality constraints → consistency.
- Cosheaf variance → arrow category → partial subdiagrams → universal object.

The polynomial is defined on total slice maps and extended to partial domain
spans after all monomorphisms have been shown to be restriction monomorphisms.
The supplement's fold construction retains its typed evaluator and uses
strength only for total program-parameter maps.
