## Appendix D

The interpretation in all models of a consistent extension of PA on the carrier of natural numbers is faithful. The model-family square commutes strictly under extension of theories. When $c$ is a restriction idempotent on $\omega$, each numeral observed at $c$ is observed at its image under the quotient functor. This is inclusion of sets. ## Appendix A

In Lemma A.7, $mr=\bar r$ fixes $f=me$. By leastness, $\operatorname{ran}(f)\le mr$. Every restriction idempotent $a$ fixing $f$ admits a splitting through which $m$ factors. This gives $mr\le a$ and, on taking $a=\operatorname{ran}(f)$, the reverse inequality. The Lean proof gives both comparisons and uses antisymmetry.
