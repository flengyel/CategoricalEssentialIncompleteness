## Proofs compared with v93

**Section 1.** For $f:Y\rightharpoonup Z$ and a section $s:Z\rightharpoonup Y$, $fs=\bar s$ and $fsf=f$. Hence $fs$ fixes $f$, and $uf=vf$ implies $u(fs)=v(fs)$ for parallel $u,v:Z\rightharpoonup W$. This is the range universal property. For complementary restriction idempotents $c,d$ on $X$, the copairing is $[c,d]:X+X\rightharpoonup X$ and has range $c\vee d=1_X$. If a total $g:X\to X$ has zero inverse images of $c$ and $d$, range agreement first gives $\operatorname{ran}(g)[c,d]=0$ and then $\operatorname{ran}(g)=0$. Thus $g=0$ and $1_X=0_X$, excluding such a separator when $1_X\ne0_X$. [Section equations and ranges](https://github.com/flengyel/CategoricalEssentialIncompleteness/blob/2269c9674e710a7032e6751e7db2473147aaaf96/Lean/PCats/Ranges.lean#L168) · [Complementary copairing and zero conclusion](https://github.com/flengyel/CategoricalEssentialIncompleteness/blob/2269c9674e710a7032e6751e7db2473147aaaf96/Lean/CategoricalEssentialIncompleteness/V46DominicalDiagonal.lean#L53) · [Manuscript argument](https://github.com/flengyel/CategoricalEssentialIncompleteness/blob/2269c9674e710a7032e6751e7db2473147aaaf96/manuscript/latex/sections/01-introduction.tex#L193)

**Lemma A.7.** In the regular category of total morphisms, factor $f=me$ with $e:X\twoheadrightarrow I$ a regular epimorphism and $m:I\hookrightarrow Y$ a monomorphism. Let $r:Y\rightharpoonup I$ be a partial inverse of $m$ in the restriction category. The idempotent $mr=\bar r$ fixes $f$, giving $\operatorname{ran}(f)\le mr$. For every restriction idempotent $a$ fixing $f$, choose a splitting $a=i_a s_a$ with $i_a:Y_a\to Y$ total and $s_a:Y\rightharpoonup Y_a$. The morphism $s_a f$ is total. The image property gives a unique $u:I\to Y_a$ with $i_a u=m$, so $am=m$ and $a(mr)=mr$. Taking $a=\operatorname{ran}(f)$ gives the reverse inequality. The independent applications check both orders and the unique factor through the splitting. **Proposition C.1.** Write $K$ for the halting set. In the paired evaluator the ordinary program indexed by $e$ supplies the partial recursive extension, and the program indexed by $m$ tests its allowed domain relative to $K$. The separator $A$ tests the ordinary diagonal for even output. One total recursive $h$ works for **all** $n,m$ in $n\in K\iff\langle h(n),m\rangle\in A$. The two machine-diagonal domains also impose their oracle-domain test. For **every** partial $s:\mathbb N\rightharpoonup\mathbb N+\mathbb N$ having left inverse image $A^c$ and right inverse image $A$, these inverse images cover the source. A lift would give an ordinary total recursive function whose output tag decides $A$, whatever its value within its summand. This contradicts the reduction from $K$. The statement applies to the specified section and all the other candidates.
