# Manuscript outline and mathematical writing

The abstract states the separation criterion, identifies the free theory, gives the equivalence for constant families, and states the uniform arithmetic consequence. Keep the two theorem references. A reader can recover the assumptions on syntax, deduction and the theorem point from §1 and the cited statements. Listing these assumptions on p. 1 would repeat pp. 4–5 without clarifying the difference among the three applications. ## Two passages to edit

**Appendix E.5, p. 100.** After introducing $E:\mathcal D/U\to\mathcal D$, write “Let $B:\mathcal D\to\mathcal D/U$ be the restriction of $B_U$ to total morphisms. Write $e:EB\cong 1_{\mathcal D}$ for the projection isomorphism, and $j:S\hookrightarrow\mathsf{Fm}$ for the sentence inclusion.” This matches the proof given in §5.3 and gives the displayed composite its type on first use in the guide. [Appendix E.5](../../../manuscript/latex/appendices/e-reading-lean-proofs.tex#L138) · [Proof of Theorem 5.3](../../../manuscript/latex/sections/04-theory-extensions.tex#L287)

**Lemma 6.4, pp. 31–32.** Give the second clause its present proof's explicit meaning at the statement. One possible wording is “Let $s>0$, let $D_0,\dots,D_{s-1}$ be $\Sigma_1$ formulas, and let $L(x,w)$ be a $\Sigma_1$ graph for the selector program. The same conclusion holds for the entry condition $\bigvee_{j<s}(L(x,\bar j)\land D_j(x))$.” Move the existing displayed definition there and refer to it in the proof. This preserves the supplied graph, including in nonstandard models of PA.
