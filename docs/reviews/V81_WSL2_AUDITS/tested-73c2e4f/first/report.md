- **§1, lines 166–171.** Three theorems require Turing completions of the given uniformly generated isotypical B-sharp subcategory. Heller 9.2 assumes a locally connected iteration category. Lengyel 21 assumes a separated iteration category. Corrected Lengyel 26 assumes a faithful iteration functor into a separated iteration category. For the relative case the construction must supply a weak evaluator and a total index for each computation. Conversion by the frame isomorphism must give, for each partial morphism `v : V × X ⇀ X`, a total `j : V → X` with `ζ (j × 1) = v`, including equality of domains. Applying the relative result to the identity would supply the absolute case. The three manuscript examples construct their evaluators directly.
