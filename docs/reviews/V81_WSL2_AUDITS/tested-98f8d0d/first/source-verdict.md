## Mathematical and editorial findings

**The introduction omits a hypothesis in its description of Di Paola–Heller 8.15.** Sections and coproducts alone allow the one-object, one-morphism dominical category. Its sole morphism is total and is a Turing morphism of the required type. Its identity equals its zero. On the unique object, both diagonal inverse images are the same idempotent, and the top pair is complementary and bounds them. Thus the pair has a separator. The missing inference in the usual contradiction is from “the identity equals zero” to falsity. A nonzero identity on the Turing object supplies it. The affected sentences are at §1, lines 173–175. The paper uses the nonzero condition in its Corollary 3.3. All downstream bounds for the arithmetic, Miller and fork examples use their respective nonzero objects. The one-morphism model and the positive result under the repaired hypotheses both have Lean proofs. [Printed attribution](https://github.com/flengyel/CategoricalEssentialIncompleteness/blob/98f8d0d50a0cc08885d07ab4d71ec6f6a7adf885/manuscript/latex/sections/01-introduction.tex#L173) · [Counterexample](https://github.com/flengyel/CategoricalEssentialIncompleteness/blob/98f8d0d50a0cc08885d07ab4d71ec6f6a7adf885/Lean/CategoricalEssentialIncompleteness/V46TrivialDominicalCategory.lean#L103) · [Repaired diagonal argument](https://github.com/flengyel/CategoricalEssentialIncompleteness/blob/98f8d0d50a0cc08885d07ab4d71ec6f6a7adf885/Lean/CategoricalEssentialIncompleteness/V46DominicalLiteratureChecks.lean#L23) · [Corollary 3.3](https://github.com/flengyel/CategoricalEssentialIncompleteness/blob/98f8d0d50a0cc08885d07ab4d71ec6f6a7adf885/manuscript/latex/sections/03-sections-and-inseparability.tex#L110)

**Section 8.3 leaves the force of “A recursive function takes an index” implicit.** Read as the defining condition on a presentation, the paragraph is correct. Read as an existence claim about every fixed consistent presentation, it includes a consistent complete decidably axiomatized theory with the empty set of additional axioms. That theory decides every sentence, so an independent sentence at that index cannot exist. A precise replacement is “A presentation is effectively extensible if some recursive function takes an index for each consistent c.e. extension by additional axioms to a sentence independent of that extension.” Theorem 8.6 constructs one morphism in the specified PA category before quantifying over consistent fibres. It supplies precisely the PA instance. [Printed introduction to §8.3](https://github.com/flengyel/CategoricalEssentialIncompleteness/blob/98f8d0d50a0cc08885d07ab4d71ec6f6a7adf885/manuscript/latex/sections/08b-uniform-independent-sentences.tex#L4) · [Complete theory](https://github.com/flengyel/CategoricalEssentialIncompleteness/blob/98f8d0d50a0cc08885d07ab4d71ec6f6a7adf885/Lean/CategoricalEssentialIncompleteness/V46EffectiveExtensibility.lean#L23) · [Uniform sentence morphism](https://github.com/flengyel/CategoricalEssentialIncompleteness/blob/98f8d0d50a0cc08885d07ab4d71ec6f6a7adf885/Lean/CategoricalEssentialIncompleteness/V46ArithmeticUniformIndependent.lean#L204)
