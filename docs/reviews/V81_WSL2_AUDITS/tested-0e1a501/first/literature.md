# Primary references and their use

The [reading-order crosswalk](assertion-proof-crosswalk.md) retains the reference keys at each occurrence. The following identifies the mathematical role of the cited results at proof commit `0e1a501b3207ea64a3eb23740405f04b9aaa424f`. The local links identify proof inputs and applications. An attribution in the related-work section has a different logical role from an existence claim on which a construction depends.

Appendix A cites parameterized lists, finite many-sorted syntax, alternative treatments of binding and rule closure. Its data include specified initial algebras. Neither the generic generation theorem nor the substitution theorem concludes that all regular extensive categories possess these algebras. In §§6, 8–10 separate constructions supply the algebras for the examples. The proofs actually used by the manuscript are the two-sorted folds, pullback of success, image closure and algebra uniqueness at arbitrary parameter and axiom objects. The references to the historical systems of Hilbert and to the general theory of inductive definitions identify the corresponding choices of inference rules.
