## Lengyel, Theorem 4.1

The 2006 report gives a range functor from a recursion category with coproducts and ranges to a locally connected split range restriction category with coproducts, zeros and weak choice. It further assumes a weak Turing morphism $t:X\times X\to X$, a nonzero image of the identity on $X$ and an isomorphism $b:X\to X+X$. It concludes that the inverse images of the summand idempotents under $bt\Delta_X$ are recursively inseparable in the source. Its list of hypotheses contains no requirement that a switched section in the target lift to a source morphism. The proof on p. 11 reduces to the identity functor, constructs a section of the reversed restricted codiagonal and then takes a source index for its composite with the inverse of $b$. The report’s projection $p_1$ is the second projection. The TeX gives the weak Turing condition in lines 369–385 and Theorem 4.1 and its proof in lines 871–942. [CUNY record of the 2006 report](https://academicworks.cuny.edu/gc_cs_tr/277/)

The evaluator in Appendix C has a single ordinary index for values paired with a literal oracle transition-machine index for its domain. Specializing its Turing property to $u\pi_2$, for an arbitrary partial endomorphism $u$, gives precisely the total parameter and product equation of the 2006 definition. The parity isomorphism supplies $b$. Partial functions split idempotents through their domains. Singleton partial identities detect equality of partial functions. Partial monomorphisms have a partial inverse on the image. These verify the three target properties used in the earlier theorem. In the source, the complementary pair $1_A,1_{A^c}$ bounds the two diagonal inverse images. Every lift of a target morphism with reversed fibers would send those fibers to opposite tags, deciding $A$. The 2006 inseparability conclusion therefore fails for this data. The checked application to $u\pi_2$ and both diagonal conclusions identify the specific failure underlying Appendix C’s historical statement. ## Di Paola and Montagna, Corollary 4.4

The 1991 facsimile states Corollary 4.4 at the foot of p. 657. Its conclusion is failure of local connectedness for every unprimed $S_T$ in the family of consistent computably enumerable extensions of PA. The proof continues on p. 658. The authors use their Theorem 4.3 in two cases. Without $\Sigma_1$-soundness, zero is the only connected domain. With $\Sigma_1$-soundness, every nonzero connected domain lies at a standard numeral, where the proof-defined endomorphism agrees with the identity. The following Theorem 4.5, which equates local connectedness with $\Sigma_1$-soundness, concerns the primed construction. Appendix D cites the unprimed corollary with its full theory range. [1991 journal paper](https://doi.org/10.2307/2274707) · [Appendix D](../../../../../manuscript/latex/appendices/d-faithful-family.tex#L61)

In Lean, `connected_le_numeral` places every nonzero connected idempotent of the number object below a numeral idempotent. The incompleteness argument in `indistinguishable_on_connected` supplies two unequal program morphisms whose composites agree on every connected idempotent. The contradiction to local connectedness lifts to the formal-object presentation. The concluding application accepts a theory extending PA, its consistency and recursive enumerability of its axioms. I checked it at that type without a soundness assumption. The primed composition obstruction in §6 remains a separate statement.
