## Outline and mathematical obligations

The introduction states the inseparable-pair criterion for a freely generated theory. Section 2 fixes the restriction, coproduct, range and Turing structure. Section 3 diagonalizes a lifted conditional. Section 4 constructs the sentence theory, changes interpretations and presentations, proves the free adjunction, and recovers the criterion. Section 5 passes to partial maps in slices over theories. Sections 6 and 7 construct syntax in a PA-provability quotient and interpret it in arithmetic models. Section 8 compares syntactic, indexed and numerical arithmetic proofs. Section 9 replaces one oracle by all binary names of a fixed Miller sequence. Section 10 builds a computable diagram category and its endpoint examples. Appendix A constructs syntax, the capture test and the image of finite derivations. Appendix B supplies the diagram category, its evaluators and its splittings. Appendix C exhibits failure of lifting. Appendix D tests all model interpretations and theory inclusions. Appendix E describes formal proof applications and hypotheses.

## Formal comparison criteria fixed before inspection

Every paragraph and displayed formula in the companion ledger requires a witness of its stated kind. A conditional formal theorem has the right scope when its extra parameters are precisely assumptions in the corresponding text. A concrete construction or use of a theorem of the literature instead requires an actual construction or theorem application with discharged premises. A result about a model of an interface, an existential without the stipulated witness, a program-existence theorem without the printed PA proof, or a standard-number calculation cannot alone establish the corresponding model-, code-, categorical- or nonstandard-quantified claim. Named reference lines in Appendix E are evidence about what those lines establish; their branch, commit and actual types require inspection at the formal comparison stage.
