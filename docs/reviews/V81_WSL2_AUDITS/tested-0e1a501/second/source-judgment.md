## Findings in the manuscript

**Ambiguity in §8.3, lines 4–8.** The opening introduces a fixed presentation of a consistent theory and then says that a recursive function produces an independent sentence for every consistent c.e. addition. On the definition reading this describes effective extensibility. On the existence reading it supplies a witness from consistency alone. A complete consistent decidable theory, such as the theory of a specified one-element structure in the equality language, with the empty set of additional axioms, has no independent sentence. Theorem 8.6 constructs one total morphism for the specified PA presentation, subject to consistency only at each selected fiber. Introducing the second sentence with “A presentation is effectively extensible if there is a recursive function that …” gives the intended quantifier and retains the theorem. [Opening of §8.3](https://github.com/flengyel/CategoricalEssentialIncompleteness/blob/0e1a501b3207ea64a3eb23740405f04b9aaa424f/manuscript/latex/sections/08b-uniform-independent-sentences.tex#L4-L12) · [Theorem 8.6 and its single witness](https://github.com/flengyel/CategoricalEssentialIncompleteness/blob/0e1a501b3207ea64a3eb23740405f04b9aaa424f/Lean/CategoricalEssentialIncompleteness/V46ArithmeticUniformIndependent.lean)

**Bibliographic error in §8, lines 239–241.** Di Paola and Heller introduce their list on p. 594. The implication numbered (3), concerning strong definability of the recursive sets and recursive inseparability of theorems and refutations, begins on p. 595. The local theorem `ArithmeticSyntax.supports_recursivelyInseparable_of_definability` assumes consistency and numeralwise positive and negative proofs for each recursive set. It yields the asserted inseparability. The page locator is the only correction to this inference.
