## Visser’s reduction

The ninth item concerns the unrestricted statement of Visser’s Lemma 23. The first reader classified it as historical. In the project, `Janiczak.FOL.essentiallyUndecidable_of_inseparable` specializes its key inference to an equivalence-relation language and to the class-size reduction. I checked the general statement separately against the pinned dependencies. The proof in the isolated file is identical to the theorem body in the sealed second reader’s application file. It requires exactly three explicit mathematical imports for the supplied derivation. [Primary lemma](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00153-024-00911-y) · [Isolated Lean theorem](VisserReduction.lean) · [Elaborated type, axioms and dependency pins](visser-reduction-receipt.json)

| Import | Required interface |
|---|---|
| `CategoricalEssentialIncompleteness.NumericalInseparability` | `Numerical.RecursivelyInseparable P N` rules out any computable separator containing P and disjoint from N. |
| `Foundation.FirstOrder.LK.Basic` | Sentence types and their proof calculus, `Theory.Proof.weakerThan_of_le`, `WeakerThan.wk`, `neg_mdp` and `consistent_iff_unprovable_bot`. These transport derivations to the extension and rule out a sentence and its negation there. |
| `Mathlib.Computability.Reduce` | `ComputablePred.computable_of_manyOneReducible` pulls a decision procedure on theorem words back along the numerical code of the reduction. |

For any first-order language `L`, choose an encoding of its closed sentences. Assume `f` sends natural numbers to sentences and `n ↦ encode (f n)` is computable. Suppose `T ⊢ f n` on `P`, `T ⊢ ∼(f n)` on `N`, and `P,N` are recursively inseparable. Fix a consistent extension `U`. A decider for codes of `U`-provable sentences would decide `D = {n ∣ U ⊢ f n}`. Injectivity of the encoding identifies its preimage even when some natural numbers encode no sentence. Inclusion of the theorems of `T` in those of `U` gives `P ⊆ D`. A point of `D ∩ N` would yield two opposite derivations in `U`, so `D ∩ N = ∅`. This contradicts inseparability. The theorem quantifies over every consistent extension, including ones whose theorems need not be computably enumerable. The isolated check used one thread, a 3,100 MiB Lean memory limit, and only `propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound`. [Exact proof using inclusion of axiom sets](VisserReduction.lean) · [Independent receipt](visser-reduction-receipt.json)

The original audit proof expresses extension by `T ⊆ U` for the two axiom sets and obtains inclusion of their theorems by weakening. An additional version checks the more general hypothesis `{φ ∣ T ⊢ φ} ⊆ {φ ∣ U ⊢ φ}` directly, with the same conclusion, dependencies and permitted axioms. It transports provability directly by this inclusion. This includes equivalent axiom presentations and presentations extending a theory only by theorem inclusion.
