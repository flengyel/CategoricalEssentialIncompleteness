## Abstract and introduction

At abstract 4–15 the objects are domains of the *restriction* completion. An index is a generalized element from any W. The consistency restriction matters on both sides of the proposed free construction. Essential incompleteness quantifies over *all* consistent deductively closed subobjects in the fixed language. The theorem asks for both implications. Lines 17–23 assert a natural hom-set bijection under regularity, extensive finite coproducts, split monos, decidable variable equality, parameterized syntax/lists and initial proof algebras for every axiom subobject. Lines 25–32 assert Turing structure and essential incompleteness in the constant family over the carrier U of the theory u. Evaluating an arbitrary closed slice extension at the theorem point is the nonformal direction. Lines 34–44 require a single total syntactic morphism independent of the choice of consistent enumeration; a single oracle machine for the axioms on *every* representation of one Miller sequence; and two individual endpoint results allowing an arbitrary c.e. consistent extension there.

Introduction 3–72 gives the three reductions. The regular image of the finite-proof sentence conclusion is the generated theory. A Boolean interpretation lifts a separator to a consistent complete closed theory. A total reduction pulls back a complete consistent extension to a separator. The slice has both postcomposition on a theory inclusion and evaluation at a chosen global theorem. Lines 74–95 require a lift with exactly the swapped summand supports and a natural coproduct comparison, no product-preservation assumption on the range functor. Lines 97–123 fix PA for the syntactic code category, choose another model K for the axioms, use the standard model or its ultrapowers for codes, and distinguish the separate signed nullary theory. Lines 124–142 state the existence of Miller's actual sequence, an actual Bauer–Hanson topos, individual representation separators, absence of a common computable separator, and the extension result. Lines 144–157 require both the non-equivalence of restriction categories and the conservative enrichment. Related-work assertions, including those with no later use, have separate entries below.
