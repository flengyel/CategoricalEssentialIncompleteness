# Literature and imported mathematics, in first-appearance order

The bibliography is indexed by each TeX citation in `reading-order.jsonl`. The following list separates actual mathematical assertions from historical attributions and sources of definitions. A citation to a theorem in a published paper can establish what the author meant. Kernel evidence must inhabit the relevant type. Where the paper adopts a definition and proves its own particular consequence, the proof of the consequence does not automatically establish an independently asserted equivalence with the original definition.
