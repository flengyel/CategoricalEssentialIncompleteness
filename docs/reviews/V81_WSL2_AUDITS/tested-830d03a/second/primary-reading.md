# Independent reading of the sources cited in version 81

This supplement was started after the blind seal and before access to the candidate. The citations below identify which mathematical assertion has to be compared with a Lean proof. An attribution of an earlier problem, a notation, an adopted definition, and a theorem at its original type have different evidentiary requirements. The reconstruction and passage inventory give the printed locations, including proof paragraphs. The references retain their keys from the manuscript.

Our comparison must also account for logical completeness/model existence, downward Löwenheim–Skolem, least-witness searches, computability of finite proof codings, ordinary and oracle specialization, and the recursion-theoretic statements about the halting set in Appendix C. Their use does not depend on whether a bibliography key is present. In particular, an assumed model of a consistent c.e. extension cannot replace the asserted existence of such a model, and a function specified to be computable requires the corresponding witness.
