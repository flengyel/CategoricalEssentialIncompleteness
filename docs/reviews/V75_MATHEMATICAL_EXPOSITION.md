The most consequential ambiguity in v74 occurs in §6.2. Read as a statement about arbitrary numerical arguments of a constructor, its converse about postfix coding is false. ## A. A false claim about postfix words

**§6.2, before Proposition 6.5, pp. 27–28.** A constructor concatenates the words supplied for its children and appends the root. The passage gave a correct unary example and described the corresponding equivalences for a function or relation and “its ordered children.” This is correct if “children” means those recovered by the parser. Under the section’s universal-closure convention, the wording also admits a claim about the supplied words, without any parsing hypothesis. For a binary relation that converse is false. [Original passage](https://github.com/flengyel/CategoricalEssentialIncompleteness/blob/c4c6cae4ed158bcc71fd8d279eca38c8ad823b77/manuscript/latex/sections/05a-internal-syntax.tex#L341)

Take one allowed variable $v$ and an allowed binary relation symbol $R$. Supply the empty word as the first argument and the two-token word $vv$ as the second. Neither word represents one term. Their concatenation followed by $R$ is $vvR$, the valid formula $R(v,v)$. The parser recovers the children $v$ and $v$. In particular, recognizing the result does not certify the proposed division of its word.

There are two valid assertions. Words for children of the indicated sorts can be concatenated and followed by their root. Conversely, parsing an accepted word produces its ordered children, each of the required sort. For a parsed relation node with label $r$ and children $t_1,\ldots,t_k$, the carrier condition is $\Pi_k(r)\land\bigwedge_j\operatorname{Tm}(t_j)$. The constructors in Proposition 6.5 have precisely these typed sources. Induction for the fold uses the children recovered by the parser. Neither initiality nor its stability under parameters needs a new hypothesis. [Revised proof](../../manuscript/latex/sections/05a-internal-syntax.tex)

The two directions can be checked separately in Lean. The constructor lemmas take a tuple in the polynomial carrier as an argument. The converse produces such a tuple from an accepted word. Its existential quantifier matters. [Constructor lemmas](../../Lean/CategoricalEssentialIncompleteness/V46SyntacticSyntaxParserLaws.lean#L151) · [Decomposition](../../Lean/CategoricalEssentialIncompleteness/V46SyntacticSyntaxDecomposition.lean#L157) · [Evaluation on a constructor](../../Lean/CategoricalEssentialIncompleteness/V46SyntacticSyntaxConstructorLaws.lean#L92)
