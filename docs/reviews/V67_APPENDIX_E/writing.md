# Mathematical writing review of Appendix E

Appendix E of *Logical consequence and essential incompleteness in recursion categories*, v67, pp. 86–88, needs local revisions in all six readings. The main omissions are the two inequalities in E.4 and the deductive-closure argument in E.5. The mathematical and Lean versions of an inference should explain one another. The six subsections can retain their order.

## Reading order and revisions

| Passage in v67 | Purpose and difficulty | Revision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Opening and E.1, p. 86 | Restriction and extension give the hom-set bijection. The opening uses the sentence morphisms before naming them, and E.1 describes the same composition twice more. “Substitution preserves the assignment” leaves the preserved operation unclear. | Give the composition convention beside the typed composite. Identify the forward map with restriction to atoms. In the reverse direction the variable map fixes variables, and the interpretations of nullary predicates are closed. The resulting interpretation commutes with admissible substitution. Retain the two inverse laws, the unit and the counit. |
| E.2, p. 86 | The proof goes from a hypothetical complete extension to a separator, then from a separator to a complete extension. The last sentence invokes the extension property before establishing that the true sentences extend the generated theory. | Explicitly assume the disjointness of the pair and consistency of the generated theory. Truth of the signed axioms and preservation of truth by the rules give the inclusion. Negation interchanges the two truth values, giving consistency and completeness. Connect these facts to the arguments supplied to `h.2`. |
| E.3, p. 87 | The two derivations require one valuation. The model in which the support list is formed first appears after the induction. “The consistency hypothesis for the arithmetic diagonal pair” can attach consistency to the pair. | Introduce an arbitrary model of PA and its coded derivations before defining the list. Name the signed theory as the object of the consistency assertion. Preserve the contrast between finite support in the model and a separator for the whole pair. |
| E.4, p. 87 | The lifted conditional interchanges the inverse images of the two summand domains. The expression “the two given inclusions” omits the inputs to the application of antisymmetry. | Recall the range functor and identify the two diagonal domains. With $E=Fg$, display $E^*N=E^*F(a_0)\le E^*P$ and $E^*P=E^*F(a_1)\le E^*N$. The citation to Appendix C explains the lifting hypothesis. |
| E.5, p. 87 | Evaluation at a theorem point takes any closed extension back to a closed theory. “Initiality puts the conclusions” omits the morphism and the reverse inclusion needed for closure. | Write $E_U(W)=\lambda_U^*W$. ## Editorial basis and scope

Knuth, Larrabee, and Roberts, §1, points 10–14 and 17, discuss connecting formulas in prose, naming variables on introduction, the reader’s immediate needs, and ambiguity in reading order. These principles support the typed composite in E.1, the two displayed inequalities in E.4 and the distinction between output and code in E.6. The preference for compact proofs in the manner of Došen–Petrić is the author’s. [*Mathematical Writing*, §1, printed pp. 2–3](https://www-cs-faculty.stanford.edu/~knuth/papers/cs1193.pdf#page=4)

Delete the announcements “use different facts” in E.1 and “the order of the first two proof steps gives the uniformity” in E.6. Preserve operative qualifiers such as *closed*, *consistent*, *total* and *internal*. Lean expressions belong wherever their arguments identify a mathematical choice or a proof obligation. The v67 appendix contains no LLM-facing instructions. Its Palomar footnotes should identify the private proof source until a versioned registration can be cited.

Scope: Appendix E and the corresponding statements and proof steps in Sections 3–5 and 8. No claim about the exposition of the rest of the manuscript or any additional Palomar verification follows from this review.

In v68 the composition convention accompanies the typed morphisms in E.1. Each Lean expression discussed in E.2–E.6 identifies a mathematical operation, witness or hypothesis. The distinction between the full signed-theory lemma and its existential consequence is preserved in the source footnote.
