# Appendix D mathematical review

I read the four parts of Appendix D at `716979153912a5b3d13fd88dd8303e8cf9017949`, with the definitions of the syntactic quotient, the model interpretation and the numerical category in §§6–8, before comparing the statements with Lean. The appendix assumes a consistent extension of PA in the fixed arithmetic language. Computable enumerability enters the non-local-connectedness assertion and enumeration of proofs of numeral instances. Theorem D.1 and Corollary D.2 apply without it.

**The family and Theorem D.1.** Completeness provides a model of the consistent theory. Downward Löwenheim–Skolem in the countable language gives a countable model, which is infinite since it satisfies PA. Transport yields a structure on the underlying set of natural numbers. If two program classes differ, adjoining the negation of their universal graph equivalence is consistent. The same argument gives a model on that carrier distinguishing the graphs. The recursive object codings are bijections, so different numerical graphs give different interpreted partial maps of the prescribed type. Lean constructs the countable model, proves completeness for this family and obtains faithfulness. [Countable models](../../../Lean/CategoricalEssentialIncompleteness/CountableArithmeticModels.lean#L73) · [Interpreted object codings](../../../Lean/CategoricalEssentialIncompleteness/V46PrintedObjectCodingModels.lean#L153) · [Faithful family](../../../Lean/CategoricalEssentialIncompleteness/SyntacticFamily.lean#L43)

Restrictions, zeros, ranges, products, coproducts and finite joins are computed coordinatewise. Every restriction idempotent splits through its family of supports. A singleton supported at one coordinate is connected and detects both definedness and the value at that point. Testing a partial monomorphism on two such singleton maps proves injectivity on each component domain, so the partial inverses on the component images give a section. Inverse image distributes over binary union. Each object term is built from a nonempty carrier by products and sums. The nonempty model family thus distinguishes the identity from zero on every interpreted term. The family restriction lattices are powersets of the disjoint unions of the component carriers, so a disjoint pair has a separator. The source switch lemma gives the stated lift for complementary source idempotents. The intervening assertion about failure of local connectedness uses consistency and computable enumerability of the PA extension. Lean proves it for the unprimed category on formal object terms. **Theory extension and Corollary D.2.** A proof of graph equality in the smaller theory is a proof in the larger theory. The representative of the range is given by existential quantification of the input in either theory. The chosen operations and evaluator act on the same indices. At every model of the larger theory, both composites in the square have the same object assignment and the same graph on each morphism. Lean proves equality of the two composite functors, the identity equations and both composition equations. Every target class has a representative in the source. Factoring that representative through an arbitrary source Turing morphism and mapping the total parameter gives the target factorization. Preservation of restrictions, composition, the diagonal and the two summand idempotents gives the two image identities. Applying §7 in the target proves inseparability; this is needed because a separator in the target need not descend. Every partial function is available at each model coordinate. At one chosen coordinate take the constant zero of that model on the complement of the evaluator’s diagonal domain, and nowhere else in the family. A total parameter value at this coordinate would make its diagonal application defined exactly when it is undefined. **Numeral instances.** Provable equivalence of representatives gives exactly the same provable numeral instances. Proof enumeration gives a c.e. set. Order preservation follows by instantiating the provable implication. A proof of a conjunction gives proofs of both conjuncts and conversely, so the observation of a meet is an intersection. Top holds at every numeral and consistency gives empty bottom. The graph obtained by composing with the constant at a numeral has its sole possible output that numeral, subject to its domain predicate. Equality with the total constant therefore expresses exactly provability of the numeral instance. Faithfulness detects this equality on the family, and soundness and completeness for the models on the fixed carrier identify it with truth in every such model. Under an extension the proved instances form a subset of the new ones. The v90 correspondence table called this equality. The manuscript and the Lean declaration both give inclusion. **True arithmetic.** Every numerical partial recursive map on a given pair of types has a program. Two programs for the same typed map have equivalent graphs on every numerical input and output because the object codings are bijections. That sentence belongs to true arithmetic precisely when it holds in the standard model. The induced hom-set bijections preserve identities and composition and use the common object terms. Lean supplies inverse functors and identifies the value of the forward functor with the standard interpretation. The consistency and completeness of true arithmetic are compatible with the inseparability in D.2, which has no enumerability assumption.
