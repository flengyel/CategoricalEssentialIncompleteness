# Di Paola–Montagna, Corollary 4.4

Appendix D cites the correct corollary, with the correct hypotheses and page range. I inspected the journal facsimile of Robert A. Di Paola and Franco Montagna, *Some properties of the syntactic p-recursion categories generated by consistent, recursively enumerable extensions of Peano arithmetic*, JSL 56 (1991), 643–660. Corollary 4.4 appears at the foot of printed p. 657, and its proof ends in the first paragraph of p. 658. These are pages 16 and 17 of the archived PDF. [Journal article](https://doi.org/10.2307/2274707) · [Facsimile identity and page map](../V81_WSL2_AUDITS/tested-0e1a501/first/primary-sources-receipt.json)

On p. 646 the authors identify indices in the unprimed category when $T$ proves universally that the programs have the same domain and agree wherever defined. Section 4 on p. 656 tests equality of morphisms by precomposition with connected domains. Corollary 4.4 applies to the unprimed category for every consistent computably enumerable extension of PA. In the case of a theory that fails $\Sigma_1$-soundness, Theorem 4.3 leaves zero as the only connected domain. In the sound case each nonzero connected domain is a numeral singleton. The authors exhibit a partial identity whose totality would imply the consistency of $T$, while its restriction to each numeral singleton agrees with the identity. Both cases give failure of local connectedness. Theorem 4.5 on p. 658 concerns the primed construction.

The manuscript invokes this result only for computably enumerable $T$. The Lean theorem for the unprimed category assumes consistency and recursive enumerability of the theory extending PA.
