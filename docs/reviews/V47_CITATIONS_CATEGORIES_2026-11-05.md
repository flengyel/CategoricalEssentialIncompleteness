# Citation audit: categorical recursion and partial maps

All 16 assigned references are real, their bibliographic data are accurate, and their cited results support the manuscript's attributions. The audit found one unnecessary theorem locator and two opportunities to reduce repeated provenance. It did not establish a false mathematical attribution.

Audit date: 5 November 2026. Base manuscript: `fc47dd92e97373244f72bfe191456863e6de8e88`, with concurrent editorial changes in `manuscript/latex`. The inventory contains 49 citation-key occurrences. Line numbers below identify the editorial copy before the final integration; section names and quoted subjects identify the passages if lines move.

## A. Kill-shots / hard blockers

None established in this citation subset. In particular, Di Paola–Montagna's nonlocal-connectedness corollary concerns the unprimed category used in the manuscript, and the cited idempotent-splitting results permit the arbitrary idempotents used in the Karoubi completion.

## B. Major weaknesses

The proof of `lem:arithmetized-evaluation` cites Montagna p.108 and p.115, Note 1. These passages give formalized parameterization/Turing structure and the computation coding; they do not print each of the manuscript's uniform functionality, universal-evaluation, composition, and specialization equations as a separately numbered theorem. The attribution is accurate as provenance for the construction. It is not a source in which every displayed equation can be checked verbatim. This distinction matters when planning the Lean verification: those equations still require their own machine proofs. It does not show that the manuscript's mathematical claims are false.

## D. Cross-section consistency

- The syntactic construction, comparison, and AppD use the unprimed `S_T`. The description of `S'_T` identifies the historical pointwise proposal and then cites the separate composition correction. None of the checked DPM locators is being used to transfer an invalid primed composition theorem into the unprimed construction.
- Restriction idempotent splitting and arbitrary idempotent splitting are distinguished in the preliminaries. Cockett–Lack Proposition 2.26 and Cockett–Hofstra §3.5 cover the larger construction when it is used.
- The manuscript's agreement-law range convention is stronger than the minimal range-category axioms. AppA's regularity assumption is stated separately; the two Range Categories papers are used for different steps.
