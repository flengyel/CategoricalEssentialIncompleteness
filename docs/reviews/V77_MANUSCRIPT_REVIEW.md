# Exposition and proof details in manuscript v77

The review found two places where v76 could be read as making a false claim. In §6.3 the distinction is between a recursive test on well-formed inputs and a recursive set of numerical codes. In Lemma 8.9 the distinction is between a partial inverse in a restriction category and a total inverse in its category of total morphisms. The corrected arguments use the hypotheses already stated.

## Scope and outline

The reading covered the abstract, §§1–10 and Appendices A–E. The section and subsection order agrees with the [outline of the paper](V75_MATHEMATICAL_EXPOSITION.md#c-section-by-section-review). Sections 1–3 introduce the problem, restriction categories and diagonal comparison. Sections 4–5 construct internal theories and the partial-map categories associated with them. Sections 6–8 construct and interpret the syntactic category of arithmetic. Sections 9–10 treat Miller sequences and the three-point space. Appendices A–D give the constructions of syntax and cosheaves, the obstruction to section lifting, and the family of arithmetic model interpretations. Appendix E identifies corresponding Lean proofs. The edits preserve this order. In the abstract, the criterion now defines both essential incompleteness and the separator it excludes, before the applications use those terms.

## Two mathematical corrections

**§6.3, Proposition 6.7 and the following calculation.** V76 identified the numerical admissibility predicate with “the recursive free-for condition.” Let
$$
D(\varphi,x,t)=\operatorname{Fm}(\varphi)\land V(x)\land\operatorname{Tm}(t).
$$
The syntactic traversal gives a primitive-recursive success tag $b(\varphi,x,t)$. Admissibility as a subobject has numerical predicate $D\land(b=1)$. Its complement *in the source* has predicate $D\land(b=0)$.

To see why the qualification matters, choose one variable $v$ and let the allowed nullary relation labels be a computably enumerable nonrecursive set $A$. For any numerical label $n$, form the raw atomic word $P_n$. Substitution of $v$ for $v$ has no capture at an atom. Thus the triple $(P_n,v,v)$ belongs to the admissibility carrier exactly when $n\in A$. A decider for that carrier on arbitrary codes would decide $A$. On a well-formed input, testing the success tag suffices. The existing Lean definition explicitly includes the source predicate. [Old passage](https://github.com/flengyel/CategoricalEssentialIncompleteness/blob/e000bdc7586ee81ca5e6a090ae5061923688fb44/manuscript/latex/sections/05a-internal-syntax.tex#L631) · [Revised calculation](../../manuscript/latex/sections/05a-internal-syntax.tex) · [Lean predicate](../../Lean/CategoricalEssentialIncompleteness/V46ConcreteNamedComparisonFreeForTheory.lean#L35)

**§8.7, proof of Lemma 8.9.** V76 invoked partial inverses “in $\mathcal B_{\rm PA}$,” where $\mathcal B_{\rm PA}=\operatorname{Tot}(\operatorname{Split}(S_{\rm PA}))$. The partial inverse of a proper subobject inclusion is defined only on its image. What the proof needs in the total category is a morphism choosing a coproduct summand. Let $m:C\hookrightarrow\omega$ and $m':C'\hookrightarrow\omega$ represent the complementary subobjects. Their copairing is an isomorphism. With $i_0,i_1:\omega\to\omega+\omega$ the injections, put
$$
\ell=[i_1m,i_0m'][m,m']^{-1}.
$$
Both factors are total. The equations $\ell m=i_1m$ and $\ell m'=i_0m'$ are the two branch values required for the diagonal argument. Source and target are identity splittings, so the same morphism is total in $S_{\rm PA}$. The wording review distinguished modifiers that carry a hypothesis from those that stand in for an argument. For example, “appropriate” did not identify either carrier at the two sorts of syntax nodes. By contrast, the unrestricted and oracle-relative notions of recursiveness in Appendix C must remain distinct. The section order is unchanged.
