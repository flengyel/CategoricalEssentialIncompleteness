# Section 8.6 — functor on signed numeral instances

## Which ordinary derivations the named word predicates count

Let CΓ be `ArithmeticSentenceInseparability.theoremSet Γ` (`V46ArithmeticSentenceInseparabilityCore.lean:35`). This is the actual literal conclusion-image predicate in the standard model. There is a direct **two-way** identification with finite proofs using the printed rule presentation:

1. `V46SyntacticGeneratedAxioms.lean:31 proofs`, `:34 conclusion` use `V46SyntacticGeneratedTheory.lean:34 proofAlgebra`, which is the initial derivation algebra for the logical rules and the filtered Γ axiom inclusion. `V46SyntacticGeneratedAxioms.lean:89 literalTheoremCarrier_model` and `:81 theoremCarrier_model` assert that a word belongs to CΓ iff it is a sentence word and some standard proof point has this conclusion word.
2. `V46SyntacticFiniteDerivations.lean:34 Derives` is external finite induction: a rule label of arity i has one child proof at each prescribed ordered premise. `V46SyntacticFiniteDerivationsStandard.lean:53 standard_iff` gives derivability iff there exists just such a proof point with that conclusion. `V46SyntacticFiniteNamedDerivations.lean:51–59` specializes it to the same named Γ rules, with logical schemes, nonlogical axioms, generalization and modus ponens at lines 61, 74, 83, 95 and 110. This equivalence is at ℕ; no assertion about externally finite trees for nonstandard proof codes is needed.
3. Specialize to a closed named formula φ. Its formula point has value formulaCode φ by `V46SyntacticNamedHilbertCoordinates.lean:37,44`. Sentence membership of its word follows as in `V46SyntacticNamedHilbertRealization.lean:142–157`. Equality of the two underlying conclusion words implies equality of the formula points by subtype extensionality. Together steps 1–3 give CΓ membership iff finite named rule derivability, for both φ=sₙ and φ=¬sₙ. The latter is closed because negation preserves free variables.

Thus an extensional sentence theory may be represented by its set of standard finite-rule consequences CΓ. Equality of two source theories means equality of those sets; it never requires PA to prove equivalence of their chosen Σ₁ axiom predicates.

There is a second Lean presentation called `SyntacticNamedHilbert.Derives`. The implications `native_instance_positive` and `native_instance_negative` at `V46ArithmeticInternalComparison.lean:58,76` run **from** this relation to proof-image membership. They are not biconditionals. If an endpoint must use that separate relation explicitly, the following optional bridge gives the converse for any closed φ and any Γ extending PA. From CΓ membership extract the standard proof point above. In every arithmetic model K of `NamedGammaModel.sourceTheory Γ`, `V46NamedGammaModelSource.lean:101 source_model_models` and `V46NamedGammaSoundness.lean:45 axioms_valid` discharge the axiom hypothesis of `V46NamedPlainSoundness.lean:60 valid_conclusion`. Use `V46SyntacticNamedHilbertCoordinates.lean:44,50` to evaluate this validity at φ. Then `V46ArithmeticNamedPresentationSemantics.lean:92 sentence_semantics` proves that K satisfies the ordinary closed translation of φ. Apply first-order `Arithmetic.complete` for that source theory (equality axioms enter through PA as in `V46NamedGammaModelSource.lean:82–97`). Use `:60 source_axioms_admissible`, `V46ArithmeticNamedProofTranslation.lean:222 sentence`, and `V46ArithmeticNamedPresentationRoundtrip.lean:138 sentence_zero_roundtrip` to obtain a named proof of φ, using the **forward implication** from the translated sentence to φ. This optional bridge is a proof plan, not a located standalone biconditional. It is unnecessary when the endpoint states ordinary provability by the external finite rules above.

## The poset and functor

Use a subtype of **sets of sentence words**, ordered by inclusion. One precise presentation is the class of sets T=CΓ for some Σ₁ axiom presentation Γ which derives the chosen PA axioms and whose sentence theory is consistent. Express consistency extensionally as absence of a word and its syntactic negation in T, for sentence words. Consistency may alternatively be supplied by the existing named-system hypothesis via `V46NamedGammaEvidenceSoundness.lean:271 standard_sentence_evidence_disjoint`. The Γ witness belongs in a proposition about T, not in its identity or order. An equivalent source uses a partial-order quotient of axiom presentations by equality of their theorem sets; ordering raw presentations by inclusion of their axiom predicates is unnecessarily restrictive.

Such a source admits every ordinary consistent c.e. PA extension: take an axiom enumeration (or enumerate the whole c.e. closed theory), use a Σ₁ predicate for its standard set of codes, filter to sentences, and take its finite-rule closure. Conversely CΓ is c.e. by `V46ArithmeticSentenceInseparability.lean:37 theoremSet_enumerable`, and is the finite-rule closure by the preceding equivalence. If the final Lean statement begins with an arbitrary `REPred` enumeration instead of a Σ₁ presentation, expose the input conversion as a lemma. The c.e./Σ₁ characterization is `rePred_iff_sigma1`; the evaluation direction and unary free-variable reindexing are exemplified at `V46ArithmeticSentenceInseparability.lean:26–35`. The reverse specialization from a general `REPred` to a carrier with the original standard extension was not run or located as an already packaged declaration. Every c.e. theory itself contains PA’s theorems and can be used as its own axiom set, so no source-cofinality or effectiveness of presentation selection is required.
