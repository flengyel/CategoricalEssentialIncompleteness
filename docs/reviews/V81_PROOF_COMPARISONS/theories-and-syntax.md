No import or instantiated Lean theorem for Visser’s claim at its own types was identified. [Visser, Lemma 23](https://research-portal.uu.nl/ws/portalfiles/portal/230468330/s00153-024-00911-y.pdf)

[Kirst and Peters, Definitions 7, 17 and Theorem 20](https://www.ps.uni-saarland.de/Publications/documents/KirstPeters_2023_Goedel.pdf)
