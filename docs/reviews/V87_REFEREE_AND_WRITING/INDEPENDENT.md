# Independent reading of the v87 revisions in §§4–9

I reconstructed the edited arguments from the TeX, then compared the range and Miller arguments with the Lean sources. The changes resolve notation and attribution questions. I found no mathematical error or missing Lean obligation in the revised passages. This is a review of the reported passages, not a fresh whole-paper kernel replay.

| Passage | Reconstructed argument and result |
|---|---|
| §4 | For a split target and a restriction idempotent $q$ on $E_d$, $qr_d=r_d\overline{qr_d}$. Thus $m_dqr_d=F(d)\overline{qr_d}$ is a restriction idempotent on $FY$. If $q$ fixes the extended morphism, $m_dqr_d$ fixes $F(f)$ by the support equations. Leastness of the range upstairs, followed by restriction to $E_d$, gives $r_d F(\operatorname{ran} f)m_d\le q$. This proves the previously compressed inequality. The other inequality follows because that idempotent receives the extended morphism. Lean’s `chosenSplitExtension_range` proves the same equality by range agreement. Renaming the ambient right adjoint leaves the inference endofunctors distinct. The transpose belongs to the target initial algebra with carrier $P_{L(A)}$ throughout. |
| §5 | Before Theorem 5.2 the author assumes initiality for every axiom subobject of the constant sentence family in every parameter slice. Theorem 5.3 retains this condition via its reference to the categorical and syntax hypotheses of Theorem 5.2. Its additional logical theorem point remains explicit and enables evaluation back in the base. |
| §6 | The bounded evaluator’s stage and the two traces have distinct names. The program index in the construction of the specialization function is fixed while the two traces are searched for. The finite selector family in Lemma 6.4 still requires disjunction over precisely its standard finite set of outputs. The derivation initiality lemma is over formulas. In §8 the iterated slice equivalence transports the inference functor over the parameterized formula object, which is what supplies the required initial algebra in each parameter slice. |
| §7 | $\operatorname{Th}(K)$ is a subset of $G_{\mathbb N}S$ consisting of codes whose decoded sentences hold in the chosen model $K$. It enters the standard soundness factorization and its ultrapower through its inclusion. The latter has codomain $G_M S$ after composition with the comparison isomorphism. This construction carries neither a truth predicate of the nonstandard arithmetic model nor an implication from consistency alone to soundness. The renaming of the graph morphism in the naturality proof leaves the common c.e. axiom set fixed. |
| §8 | The numerical domains $D_0,D_1$ are sets. Their partial identities are morphisms in Rec. The syntactic restriction idempotents are in the PA category, and their splitting objects $A_i=(\omega,a_i)$ belong to its splitting. These have different types. The enlarged sets of provable and refutable numeral instances still contain the corresponding $D_i$; they differ both from derivation carriers and from the sets of codes of all provable and refutable sentences. Bernardi gives the PA-provable equality example on p. 125 and the distinct-class assertion on p. 126, Proposition 2(b). A separator of disjoint nonempty unions would separate chosen classes in them. The article need not use the paper’s graph-formula encoding. |
| §9 | For one program index $d$ and real $r\in[0,1]$, every oracle naming $a$ must give, at each precision $k$, a rational approximation to $r$. The rational index may depend on the oracle and on $k$. Miller identifies the stronger coordinate equation immediately after the proof of Theorem 6.3. Bauer and Hanson use precisely the interleaved binary representation. Definition 2.1 allows arbitrary reals, but the singleton-interval assertion in §2.2.1 requires the unit interval. A constant name for 2 shows why the restriction matters in reading their Theorem 2.2. The revised manuscript explicitly retains it. The coordinate of the fixed point uses the same machine index as the interval at that coordinate. Lean’s `IsMiller`, `exists_isMiller` and `miller_coordinate_of_approximation` have these quantifiers for the fixed coding. In the final corollary the point coding $0=0$ witnesses the theorem-point hypothesis. A complementary separator in the slice would yield a total Boolean extension by assigning opposite truth values to its summands. This justifies shortening the repeated proof. |

I checked the revised PDF at pp. 13, 50, 58, 60 and 68 and followed the active TeX cross-references. The source contains 161 distinct labels, 77 numbered results and three numbered definitions. Every reference resolves, including the renamed subsection in §9. The original papers used for the source checks are [Bernardi](https://doi.org/10.1090/S0002-9939-1986-0848889-9), [Miller](https://people.math.wisc.edu/~jsmiller8/Papers/continuous.pdf#page=13), and [Bauer and Hanson, v3](https://arxiv.org/html/2404.01256v3#S2.SS2).
