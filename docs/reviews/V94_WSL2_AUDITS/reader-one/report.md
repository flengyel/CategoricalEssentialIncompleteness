## Mathematical and source comparison

Theorem 3.1 obtains a total source endomorphism by applying the Turing property to the partial lift. Reversing the two coproduct fibers forces both inverse images to vanish. Noncoverage also requires the interpreted identity to be nonzero and inverse image along that witness to preserve this particular join. Corollary 3.3 deduces source inseparability from total source switches for complementary pairs together with preservation of binary joins under total inverse images in the target. [Comparison proofs](https://github.com/flengyel/CategoricalEssentialIncompleteness/blob/b5db36ed4dd2f2b5bfe48f746ff167a95a8a7182/Lean/CategoricalEssentialIncompleteness/V46Comparison.lean#L36)

In Lemma 5.1 the recursion category on the constant projections over the theorem object has a restriction equivalence from its idempotent completion to the whole partial-map category of the slice. In Theorem 5.2 a monic theory inclusion gives postcomposition fully faithful, with both adjunctions $Σ_i ⊣ i^* ⊣ Σ_i$ on the underlying categories. Theorem 5.3 quantifies over all closed consistent extensions in the slice, allowing the theory to vary with the base point. [Both restriction directions](https://github.com/flengyel/CategoricalEssentialIncompleteness/blob/b5db36ed4dd2f2b5bfe48f746ff167a95a8a7182/Lean/CategoricalEssentialIncompleteness/V46ConstantSliceFull.lean#L181) · [Adjunctions](https://github.com/flengyel/CategoricalEssentialIncompleteness/blob/b5db36ed4dd2f2b5bfe48f746ff167a95a8a7182/Lean/CategoricalEssentialIncompleteness/V46PartialSliceAdjunctions.lean#L67)
