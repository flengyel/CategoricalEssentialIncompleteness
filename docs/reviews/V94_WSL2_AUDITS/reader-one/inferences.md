# Mathematical inputs at their applications

The [source register](sources.json.gz) gives the individual citation uses. The [passage map](passage-correspondence.json.gz) and [Lean proof steps](lean-proof-steps.json.gz) also cover assertions with no bibliographic footnote. The following arguments require attention to the hypotheses of an input or to the construction of a witness.

| Passage | Required inference | Application at the tested revision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| §1.1, §3 | A section of $f$ gives a range. A Turing parameter for a fixed-source partial morphism gives a total diagonal index. With complementary bounds, both inverse images vanish. A nonzero identity excludes the resulting zero total morphism. | `IsSection.isRange`, `DominicalDiagonal.fixed_source_diagonal` and `DominicalDiagonal.zero_of_empty_preimages` prove these steps. `TrivialDominicalCategory.diagonal_pair_has_separator` treats the zero identity. [Dominical comparison](https://github.com/flengyel/CategoricalEssentialIncompleteness/blob/b5db36ed4dd2f2b5bfe48f746ff167a95a8a7182/Lean/CategoricalEssentialIncompleteness/V46DominicalDiagonal.lean) |
| §2, §4 | Product preservation does not follow from the range-functor axioms. A proper invariant subobject need not omit a global point. | The doubling construction preserves the prescribed coproduct but its product comparison on nonempty sets misses unequal Boolean coordinates. In a nontrivial regular group action, the singleton summand contains every global point of the coproduct and its inclusion is proper. [Doubling functor](https://github.com/flengyel/CategoricalEssentialIncompleteness/blob/b5db36ed4dd2f2b5bfe48f746ff167a95a8a7182/Lean/CategoricalEssentialIncompleteness/DoublingRangeFunctor.lean#L129) · [Group action](https://github.com/flengyel/CategoricalEssentialIncompleteness/blob/b5db36ed4dd2f2b5bfe48f746ff167a95a8a7182/Lean/CategoricalEssentialIncompleteness/V46RegularActionPoints.lean#L76) |
| §5, §6 | Theory inclusions are monic. For the numerical carrier example an infinite c.e. subset of ℕ needs a computable injective enumeration and its partial recursive inverse. The syntactic graph calculus requires a bounded deterministic computation test and parameter specialization that PA proves correct. | `PartialSliceChangeBase.right_unit_total_iff` characterizes when the unit is total. `EnumerableCarrierEnumeration.infinite_enumerable_carrier` constructs the enumeration through first halting stages. `UniformTrace.arithmetized_evaluation` and `specialization_PA` supply the graph operations. [Enumeration and inverse](https://github.com/flengyel/CategoricalEssentialIncompleteness/blob/b5db36ed4dd2f2b5bfe48f746ff167a95a8a7182/Lean/CategoricalEssentialIncompleteness/V46EnumerableCarrierEnumeration.lean#L198) |
| §§7–8 | Standard sentence proofs from a consistent arithmetic extension have a sound interpretation in a model of its axioms. Transfer to an ultrapower applies to the first-order assertion at a finite tuple of representatives, including its negative instance. | `NamedPlainSemantics.valid_sentenceConclusion` evaluates the actual derivation. `NamedGammaEvidenceSoundness.consistent_standard_word_disjoint` supplies disjointness for the positive and negative sentence programs. `ArithmeticUltrapower.eval_representative` proves the required transfer by induction including quantifiers. [Ultrapower formula evaluation](https://github.com/flengyel/CategoricalEssentialIncompleteness/blob/b5db36ed4dd2f2b5bfe48f746ff167a95a8a7182/Lean/CategoricalEssentialIncompleteness/V46ArithmeticUltrapower.lean#L72) |
| §8 | The same computable map sends each positive diagonal instance to a theorem and each negative one to a refutation. Any recursive upper bound disjoint from the refutations would pull back to a recursive separator. For a consistent c.e. extension, a complete theory would enumerate both sides of the resulting complementary decision. | `ArithmeticDiagonalRepresentation.printed_representation` provides the map, standard values and model bounds. `ArithmeticSentenceInseparability.no_recursive_separator_of_bounds` uses the actual inverse image, and `recursive_fiber_gap` uses the c.e. set and its c.e. complement. [Pullback proof](https://github.com/flengyel/CategoricalEssentialIncompleteness/blob/b5db36ed4dd2f2b5bfe48f746ff167a95a8a7182/Lean/CategoricalEssentialIncompleteness/V46ArithmeticSentenceInseparabilityCore.lean#L99) |
| §9 | For every point in the Hilbert cube the program-indexed multivalued interval has a closed graph and a nonempty interval value. A finite-dimensional Kakutani fixed point gives compatibility of finitely many coordinates. Compactness gives one point compatible at every coordinate. | `Miller.FixedPoint.kakutani_fixed_point` has compactness, convexity, nonemptiness and closed graph in its type. `exists_coordinate_fixedPoint` and `exists_isMiller` assemble the witness for the manuscript’s choice of rational enumeration and binary names. `RealClosedConsistency.theoryAxioms_consistent` establishes the concrete theory’s consistency through the real field. [Sequence witness](https://github.com/flengyel/CategoricalEssentialIncompleteness/blob/b5db36ed4dd2f2b5bfe48f746ff167a95a8a7182/Lean/CategoricalEssentialIncompleteness/V46MillerFixedPoint.lean#L125) · [Consistency](https://github.com/flengyel/CategoricalEssentialIncompleteness/blob/b5db36ed4dd2f2b5bfe48f746ff167a95a8a7182/Lean/CategoricalEssentialIncompleteness/V46MillerRealClosedConsistency.lean#L23) |
| §10, Appendices A–B | Parameterized syntax and proof recursion must work in each of the three fibers. Finite rule trees generate the least closed subobject.
