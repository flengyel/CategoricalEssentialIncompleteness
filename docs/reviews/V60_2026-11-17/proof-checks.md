**Positioning.** Cockett, *Categories and Computability*, §7.1 and Theorem 7.8, gives an inseparable pair in every nontrivial discrete Turing category with finite joins and credits the result to Lengyel. Its reversal of the two decisions is a close antecedent of the diagonal argument. Theorem 3.1 here adds a comparison to disjoint target bounds along a range functor with a lifted reversed conditional. Theorem 4.7, Corollary 4.8 and Theorem 5.3 concern generated closed theories and their extensions. Cockett's separate Theorem 7.14 has a different predicate hypothesis and conclusion. The author's earlier *Locally connected recursion categories*, Theorem 4.1, already states the coproduct-diagonal pair. Its proof on pp. 11–12 assumes a source index for a conditional constructed in the target. The explicit lift in 3.1 is mathematically material; the antecedent can be cited for the pair without invoking that proof.

**Set control.** For disjoint subsets of a set, the characteristic function of the positive subset is a separator. The truth fiber of the singleton valuation gives a complete consistent extension, as 4.8 predicts. Using this characteristic function in a general base would require the positive subobject itself to be complemented.

No separate comparison identifying the propositional closure operator of Appendix A.6, in the base and the slices, was identified in the current correspondence, audit imports, or candidate modules.
