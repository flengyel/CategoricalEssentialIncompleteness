## Corrections and qualifications

1. **Bernardi pinpoint:** `sections/01-introduction.tex:158` cites “Section 3, pp.125–128.” The PA-provable-equality example starts in §2, p.125, and is recalled in §3, p.127. The categorical fixed-point corollary is on p.127 and inseparability theorem on p.128. Use `\cite[Sections~2--3, pp.~125--128]{Bernardi}`. The existing prose is supported.

2. **Montagna citation scope:** `sections/05-syntactic-recursion-category.tex:86` points to pp.108 and115 Note1 for the formalized computation properties in Lemma6.1. Page108 gives universal evaluation and invokes formalized s-m-n; Note1 fixes Kleene’s predicate/output coding. Those passages do not explicitly state the full composition graph equivalence or every primitive-recursive constructor now displayed. The arithmetic argument must establish those exact identities; the citation alone is insufficient certification. This is a source-scope qualification, not a discovered counterexample.

3. **Source versions:** Cockett–Lack III Proposition4.5 is verified in the author preprint, with journal metadata verified at the publisher; the journal PDF was unavailable. Rosolini’s text is the author’s1995 retypesetting of the1986 thesis, whose titlepage and Chapter2 explicitly support the no-pinpoint historical attribution. Bernardi’s original was readable as author-uploaded paginated full text. These distinctions do not reveal a bibliographic contradiction.
