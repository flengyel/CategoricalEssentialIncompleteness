For disjoint c.e. sets $B,C$, the manuscript first cites Murwanashyaka, Pakhomov and Visser's criterion for essential undecidability of the Janiczak theory with the prescribed signs on $B,C$. It then uses their definition of a propositional-logic-like theory in §4.1 and Theorem 4.3. For **each** set $D$ containing $B$ and disjoint from $C$, adding $A_n$ for indices in $D$ and its negation for indices outside $D$ gives a complete consistent extension. The coding argument that follows in the manuscript shows that the set of its theorems is c.e. precisely when $D$ is recursive. These are two distinct inferences.
