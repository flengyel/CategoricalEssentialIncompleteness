# Appendix C mathematical review

I read Proposition C.1 and its proof at `6c1c3a82666e5669827989605f240b56ffa44059` before comparing the argument with the Lean declarations.

**The evaluator.** A morphism has a domain c.e. in $K$ and an ordinary partial recursive extension. Restricting the ordinary program to the oracle-machine domain gives precisely the intersection of their domains. For a morphism on $X\times\mathbb N$, specialization of the ordinary program supplies its values, and specialization of a single oracle program tests its domain. Pairing the resulting indices gives an ordinary total recursive parameter on $X$. In particular, applying this factorization to $u\pi_2$ gives the weak Turing hypothesis for every source endomorphism $u$. [Literal evaluator and membership](../../../Lean/CategoricalEssentialIncompleteness/V46MachineOracleTuring.lean#L39) · [Parameter and Turing property](../../../Lean/CategoricalEssentialIncompleteness/V46MachineOracleTuring.lean#L58)

**The reduction.** The even-output separator $A$ depends on running the first indexed program on its own paired input. The two diagonal domains additionally require membership in the oracle-machine domain indexed by the second coordinate. This gives $D_{a_0}\subseteq A$ and $D_{a_1}\subseteq A^c$. The ordinary simulation indexed by $h(n)$ ignores its input, runs $\varphi_n(n)$ and returns zero on convergence. Thus for every $n$ and every second coordinate $m$, $n\in K$ if and only if $\langle h(n),m\rangle\in A$. Lean takes both variables as arbitrary natural numbers. It gives the two containment proofs for the evaluator with literal oracle-machine indices. **Quantification over lifts.** The switched section chooses the right copy at each $n\in A$ and the left copy on $A^c$. For any partial function with these two summand inverse images, their union forces totality. If it had a source lift, that lift would be ordinary total recursive. Its output tag would decide $A$, regardless of the numerical value in either summand. A common upper bound for the two specified branches would restrict to each branch and hence equal the switched section everywhere. Finally, a source preimage of $F(b)^{-1}s_A$ would yield a lift of $s_A$ by postcomposition with $b$.
