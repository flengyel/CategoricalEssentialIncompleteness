## Introduction, ranges and the diagonal

For $f:Y\rightharpoonup Z$ and a section $s:Z\rightharpoonup Y$, $fs=\bar s$ and $fsf=f$. In `CategoryTheory.IsSection.isRange` the type is `IsRange (rst s) f`. The restriction law makes $\bar s$ a domain. The second section equation gives the receiving equation for $fs$. If $u,v:Z\rightharpoonup W$ agree after $f$, precomposition with $s$ gives agreement after $fs$. The theorem constructs the range from this section before introducing any operation choosing ranges. A section for every $f$ then supplies chosen ranges. The first two specialized examples exhibit these two steps at arbitrary objects. [Range construction](https://github.com/flengyel/CategoricalEssentialIncompleteness/blob/2269c9674e710a7032e6751e7db2473147aaaf96/Lean/PCats/Ranges.lean#L160)

Let $c,d:X\rightharpoonup X$ be complementary restriction idempotents. The morphism on which agreement is used in the final paragraph is $[c,d]:X+X\rightharpoonup X$. It has $\operatorname{ran}[c,d]=c\vee d$. For the total diagonal index $g$, the equality, disjointness and inverse-image calculation give $cg=dg=0$. Range agreement followed by commutation of domains gives $\operatorname{ran}(g)c=\operatorname{ran}(g)d=0$. The coproduct universal property gives $\operatorname{ran}(g)[c,d]=0$. Agreement for $[c,d]$ now gives $\operatorname{ran}(g)(c\vee d)=0$. Coverage, reception, and totality force $1_X=0_X$. The code obtains $g$ from a parameter of $s\pi_2$, with source parameter object $X$, and uses the indicated argument in `zero_of_empty_preimages`. `inseparable` has an explicit premise $1_X\ne0_X$. [Diagonal and vanishing argument](https://github.com/flengyel/CategoricalEssentialIncompleteness/blob/2269c9674e710a7032e6751e7db2473147aaaf96/Lean/CategoricalEssentialIncompleteness/V46DominicalDiagonal.lean#L74)

## A.7 and the regular image

Write $f=me$ in the total category, with $e:X\to I$ regular epic, $m:I\to Y$, $rm=1_I$ and $mr=\bar r$. The first inequality uses $\bar r f=f$ and `Ranges.least`. For the second, split the fixing domain as $js$ with $sj=1$ and $j$ total. The identity $jsf=f$ implies $sf$ total because $j$ is total. The square whose horizontal sides are $sf$ and $m$ has left side $e$ and right side the total mono $j$. The diagonal $u:I\to J$ satisfies $ju=m$. Consequently $(js)m=m$ and $mr\le js$. Taking $js=\operatorname{ran}(f)$ gives the reverse inequality. The proof term implements this last specialization directly. Only the given image mono needs a partial inverse. The strict example applies the theorem to the displayed input and obtains both inequalities of restriction idempotents. [Both inequalities and their square](https://github.com/flengyel/CategoricalEssentialIncompleteness/blob/2269c9674e710a7032e6751e7db2473147aaaf96/Lean/CategoricalEssentialIncompleteness/V46RegularImage.lean#L119)

## C.1 and the oracle computation

The two numbers supplied by the total parameter in the diagonal evaluator have different jobs. The ordinary index returns a value. The other index semidecides the domain relative to the oracle. The specialization `sectionIndex` works for every oracle and all four numerical parameters. `ordinaryDiagonal_simulation n a` calculates the ordinary value on the paired index and gives the ordinary computation on $n,n$ with constant zero output when defined. The code of `simulationCode n` depends only on $n$. The universal pair in `K_iff_simulation_mem_A` proves the reduction at every second coordinate, including nonzero choices. Likewise the two fibre hypotheses on $s:\mathbb N\rightharpoonup\mathbb N+\mathbb N$ quantify existentially over the payload and universally over inputs. For *each* such $s$ and *every* proposed source $\ell$, a lift would decide $A$ by the tag of its total ordinary recursive value. `machine_no_lift` states the result with the actual coproduct comparison for the transition-machine realization. These are the two quantified examples in `Focused.lean`; changing the payload does not affect either fibre hypothesis. ## The seven arguments in Appendix E

1. The generator pair is $(X,a,b)$, with $a,b$ subobjects on $X$. The receiving presentation and closed theory are $(P,T)$. Restriction of a translation along the atom morphism yields an admissible assignment; its free extension inverts this on both hom-sets. The naturality diagrams, unit and counit are supplied at the terms indicated in the first two links.
2. The carriers $X_a,X_b$ each map into $X$. A separator gives a Boolean-valued assignment respecting both axiom signs. Its inverse-image truth subobject is a consistent complete closed extension. In the opposite direction pull a complete consistent extension back along the atom morphism to obtain a separator.
3. The two alleged inconsistent derivations are compared on one internally finite list of hypotheses. Agreement on the signed supports defines a single valuation. Induction on each derivation gives opposite truth values for the same sentence. The arithmetic provability assertion uses PA’s finite-list operations.
4. The conditional interchanges the summand domains. Pullbacks along $Fg$ give two opposite inequalities, hence equality. Their meet is zero, and each inverse image is zero. The separate counterexample shows a tag-deciding total conditional can lack every possible source lift.
5. $B:\mathcal D\to\mathcal D/U$ takes constant families on total morphisms. Evaluation at $\lambda_U:1\to U$ has $\varepsilon:\operatorname{ev}B\cong1_{\mathcal D}$. Pullback along the inverse component gives $W_0$ at $S$ and the evaluated generated formula object at $\mathrm{Fm}$. Naturality along the sentence mono identifies its pullback with $W_0$. The evaluated rules imply closure, and axiom introduction gives the reverse inclusion. A parameter-dependent complete consistent $W$ would therefore give a complete consistent extension in the base.
6. $E$ is the number object, and $e$ is the program index. The uniform morphism evaluates at this index. Derivability of the even-output sentence causes the odd branch, which supplies the opposite derivation in PA. The reversed case excludes refutation under the same consistency hypothesis.
7. The family of slice axioms is $A$; the unique fold into an algebra $\beta$ is $\widehat\beta:D_A\to Q$. Its conclusion, constructor and uniqueness laws occur in `printedFold_conclusion`, `printedFold_constructor` and `printedFold_unique`. In the relabelling argument $m:Z\hookrightarrow S$ is any axiom mono. The recovered axiom carrier is isomorphic over the sentence object, the two rule algebras correspond, and the images of the respective sentence conclusions coincide. Hence finite provability describes the actual theorem domain. Both subtleties occur in the proof ranges that end at lines 98 and 192. [All twenty checked tag ranges](appendix-e-links.json)
