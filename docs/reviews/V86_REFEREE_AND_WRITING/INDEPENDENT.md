## Historical sources

- **Di Paola and Heller.** Definition 4.1 on p. 601 fixes the three objects of the evaluator and uses a total parameter on the first. Definition 4.2 defines a recursion category to be an isotypical dominical category with a Turing morphism. The authors assume a recursion category throughout §8 (p. 621). Definitions 6.1–6.2 on p. 612 supply a section of every map, hence its range. Definition 7.1 on p. 615 also requires total coproducts of total maps and canonical product–coproduct distributivity. Proposition 7.2(i),(iii), pp. 615–616, identifies the range of a copairing and gives inverse-image preservation of joins. Theorem 8.15 on pp. 625–626 uses the reversed conditional and this preservation, concluding that the identity equals zero. Its hypotheses permit the one-object, one-arrow category, where a separator exists. The manuscript’s explicit hypothesis `1_X ≠ 0_X` rules this out. The final §1 states the p. 621 standing recursion-category assumption and both parts of the coproduct compatibility hypothesis before starting a separate paragraph for its fixed-object assertion. That assertion assumes a dominical category, binary coproducts, sections, the displayed universal property at X, and a nonzero identity on X. It requires neither an ambient isotypical category nor the two extra product–coproduct compatibility assumptions for its range proof. This matches `V46DominicalLiteratureChecks.nonzero_turing_object`. [Original article](https://doi.org/10.2307/2274352)
- **Range calculation.** Put `r = ran(g)` and `p = [c,d] : X+X ⇀ X`. From `cg = dg = 0`, range agreement gives `cr = dr = 0`. Commutativity on `X` gives `rp = [rc,rd] = 0_{X+X,X}`. Apply range agreement to `p` with `r` and `0_X` to get `r ran(p) = 0_X`. Since `ran(p) = c ∨ d = 1_X`, both `r` and `g = rg` vanish. Totality gives `1_X = 0_X`. `V46DominicalDiagonal.zero_of_empty_preimages`, lines 77–91, takes exactly these two agreement steps and is applied by `inseparable`. Its `rangesOfChoice` and `complementary_section` construct the range and the reversed conditional. `V46TrivialDominicalCategory.diagonal_pair_has_separator` supplies the degenerate case. Range agreement is Definition 5.1, p. 609.
- **Pour-El.** I inspected the page images, including the glyphs in the two displays of Definition 8. Page 274 prints an intersection in its premise and again in the conclusion. Taken literally, the premise already makes the latter intersection empty, so the conclusion allows any sentence code. Page 272 explicitly describes the undecidable sentences as the complement of the union and requires an independent sentence in each consistent c.e. enlargement of a fixed presentation. Definitions 5 and 6(b) explain the parameters and consistency of that enlargement. The revision’s footnote reports the printed discrepancy accurately. The PA result in §8.3 supplies its own uniform morphism. [Cornell scan, printed pp. 272, 274](https://pi.math.cornell.edu/~yytran/PourEl_recursion_axiomatizable_theories.pdf)
