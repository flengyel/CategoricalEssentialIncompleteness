In §1, the two categorical results attributed to Cockett have separate proofs under the hypotheses named in the introduction. A partial inverse for the diagonal gives disjoint program-point domains. Stable joins and diagonal application give an inseparable pair. An idempotent recognizing the restriction order on program points gives a proper nonzero restriction on the terminal object. The numerical antecedent of Corollary 4.8 in §1 uses Janiczak's equivalence relation with finite class sizes prescribed or forbidden by the two c.e. sets. Foundation's classical first-order calculus supplies soundness and completeness for models with equality. The statement checks use its sentence codes and quantify over every consistent extension in that language. A recursive separator yields a model with exactly the specified finite class sizes and a decision algorithm for its complete theory. [Axioms and biconditional](../../../Lean/CategoricalEssentialIncompleteness/V46JaniczakSignedTheory.lean#L60) · [Expanded extension quantifier](../../../Lean/CategoricalEssentialIncompleteness/V46JaniczakSignedTheoryChecks.lean#L25) · [Murwanashyaka, Pakhomov and Visser, §4.4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2207.08174#page=10).
