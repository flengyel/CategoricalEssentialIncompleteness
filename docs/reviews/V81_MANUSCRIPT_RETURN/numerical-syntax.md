## The two syntax predicates in Proposition 8.8

Fix $d\in\mathbb N$, $V=(1,\mathbb N)$ and unary symbol objects $(1,\Sigma_{k,0})$, $(1,\Pi_{k,0})$ for $0\le k\le d$, with recursive supports. [Lines 292–296 of the proof](../../../manuscript/latex/sections/08c-numerical-realization.tex#L292) assert recursiveness of the subsets of **all** numerical word codes that represent terms and formulas. Write $a,b$ for the two signature polynomials in the literal postfix presentation. For $t=0,1$ define $D_t:\mathbb N\to\{0,1\}$ by
$$
 D_t(w)=\left(\bigwedge_{i<\operatorname{len}(w)}
 R(\operatorname{nth}(w,i))\right)\land
 [\operatorname{run}_{a,b}(w,1)=1+\operatorname{adjoin}(\langle w,t\rangle,0)].
$$
Brackets denote the characteristic function of equality. For a record $\langle u,j\rangle$, use the shape test for $a$ at $u$ when $j=0$, that for $b$ when $j=1$, and reject when $j>1$. A shape test requires zero at a term, formula or unit leaf. It tests the supplied support at a coefficient leaf, both projections at a product, and the branch selected by a tag of zero or one at a sum, rejecting any other tag. The variable label test always succeeds. Each shape test is total recursive by induction on the polynomial. The finite conjunction and parser equality therefore give a total recursive $D_t$. The empty word fails the parser equality in both sorts.

The Lean conclusion to check for both Boolean values `formula` is `ComputablePred (fun w : ℕ => (Postfix.plainCarrier formula a b).val.Evalb ![w])` from the $2(d+1)$ recursive support predicates. For WSL2, the induction above takes `ComputablePred` at each coefficient leaf and applies to either sort.

Two counterexamples distinguish these assertions.

1. Let $H=\{e:\varphi_e(e)\downarrow\}$ and $B=2\mathbb N\cup\{2e+1:e\in H\}$. A repetition-free enumeration of the infinite c.e. set $B$ gives an isomorphism $(1,\mathbb N)\cong(1,B)$ in $\mathcal C_{\rm rec}$, since its inverse searches for each input in that enumeration. A recursive decision procedure for $B$ would decide $H$. An isomorphism of numerical objects therefore does not suffice to establish decidability of a chosen ambient carrier.
2. Take $d=0$ and $\Sigma_{0,0}=A$ recursive with no primitive-recursive characteristic function. The one-record word $c(n)$ for a nullary function symbol with label $n$ is primitive recursive in $n$. It is a term precisely when $n\in A$. The composition of a primitive-recursive term decision procedure with $c$ would decide $A$ by primitive recursion. Thus the recursion bound for the carrier in Proposition 8.8 cannot in general be strengthened to primitive recursion.
