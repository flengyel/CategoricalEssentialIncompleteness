## Reconstruction

1. Introduction. The central equivalence concerns a *consistent generated* signed theory with nullary symbols indexed by an object and all consistent closed extensions in one language. Properness is a join strictly below the sentence object. The three realizations make different claims. In particular the arithmetic signed slice theory has a different language from arithmetic with PA axioms.
2. Preliminaries. Restriction order on domains and on parallel arrows, pullback of a domain, range and the agreement law, restricted split and full Karoubi completions are distinct. The paper's range functors preserve restrictions, images, zeros, binary restriction coproducts and consequently joins of domains. The functor X↦X×2 on partial sets exhibits failure of product preservation.
3. Comparison. With h=bτΔ, a0 and a1 are the respective summand tests. A source lift of a branch-reversed test on two disjoint upper bounds gives a total index morphism g for b⁻¹ℓπ₂. On its diagonal hg=ℓg. Thus the two preimages under Fg coincide and are disjoint. Their vanishing says the pair does not cover only with nonzero FU and join stability. An internal complementary conditional suffices to exclude a separator in the source. This is a lift for one particular arrow; faithfulness does not produce it.
4. Internal theories. Restrict the conclusion of the initial inference algebra to sentences and take its image. Negation pulls back the refutation object. Chosen splittings extend F and preserve ranges via their leastness. Disjoint interpreted proof/refutation domains plus branch bounds/lifting prove internal and interpreted incompleteness. The equivalence of generated theories with fixed points is a reflection. Logical translations preserve rule instances and admissible substitutions, giving a theory-level Galois connection. Regular change of ambient category initially only compares deductions when a map of proof objects exists. The induced right adjoint on slices is pullback of Hb along the mate of the sentence/formula morphism; this types the use of the opposite mate for the rule comparison. Nullary predicate assignments X→S_P extend uniquely via free syntax. Generator pullback excludes complete extensions for inseparable pairs; a separator yields a singleton structure and Boolean valuation, with all quantifiers erased, producing a complete consistent closed extension. Disjoint pairs are restricted to those generating a consistent theory. A proper G-set subobject may contain all global points.
5. Slices. The construction is Par(D/T,Mono), with T the theorem object. The graph of a total map into U over T embeds each slice object in T×U. S-m-n in the base supplies the slice parameter. The inclusion of theorem objects gives postcomposition on spans; monicity makes the functor fully faithful and pullback both its left and right adjoint in the underlying partial-map categories. Extension by a proper mono need not preserve the restriction terminal object and the reverse unit is partial. Constant-family base change B and evaluation E at a logical theorem have EB≅id; images, finite limits/coproducts and transported rule closure give an equivalence of essential incompleteness, even for extensions varying with the parameter.
6. Syntactic category. Equality of program representatives is a single proof of universal graph equivalence. Uniform evaluation and specialization furnish a Turing morphism; PA-provable graph composition gives ordinary composition. The total restriction completion is represented by Σ₁ predicates and provably functional, supported and total graphs. Enumeration of witnesses gives an inverse for each mono on its image and sections of provable surjections. Finite-tree evaluations give parameter-stable syntax and list algebras, and Σ₁ proof-code domains with an initial derivation algebra. Induction on finite codes is carried out *in* the theory, needed for nonstandard models. Bounded-arities and decidable variable equality are part of this realization.
7. Models. In all M⊨T one interprets code graphs with intermediate numbers and traces ranging over M, obtaining a range functor. On totals, predicate interpretation is regular and preserves coproducts. The Hilbert soundness proof uses finite standard codes interpreted in an independently chosen structure K⊨Γ. Arithmetic ultrapowers M of the standard model are a separate extension of that factorization: Łoś on codes identifies the ultrapower of G_N with G_M; an ultrapower of the externally defined standard K-validity subset is transported along this isomorphism. There is no assumption of a satisfaction predicate on arbitrary nonstandard formulas in arbitrary models of PA.
8. Arithmetic. The even/odd diagonal predicates α,β are disjoint; finite PA computations prove the corresponding positive or negative standard numeral instances. Standard truth of the *universal* implications for a c.e. extension transfers to M≡N. A Σ₁ search for a proof or refutation (opposite output tags) supplies the lift, subject to disjointness in the model. For arbitrary consistent c.e. Γ extending PA deductively there is numerical independence and internal *incompleteness*, without a general claim of PA-proof of consistency of Γ. An indexed axiom family records PA plus the sentences in W_e. The complemented numeral fiber splits the theory/derivation algebra by extensivity and initiality, proving agreement of the family's proof predicate with that fixed presentation's provability at e. One Turing specialization on (e,x), followed by θ, yields one total q whose code is independent for every consistent e. A valuation can serve two finite proofs without extending the pair on all indices.
9. Miller. Morphism means a single index computes the same partial function, including definedness, for every binary representation. Compact uniform finite covers compute unanimous oracle output. The even/odd diagonal domains are inseparable in the common category. Distances to finite unions of closed boxes, combined into weighted continuous nonnegative sums, produce a name of a real below/above 1/2 with the required diagonal behavior; Miller's index property supplies a common total reduction. At an exact half each representation's first digit gives a separator but the selected set can depend on that representation. Relative enumeration of rational bounds supplies the field theory. The preimage chosen in regular-epi descent may depend on the representation, but the returned value is constant on that fiber.
10. Three-point space. The two inclusions of the common singleton give diagrams 1←0→2 and the pushout at the whole space, with effective data only at the three displayed components. Endpoints preserve the Turing evaluator; the central projection forgets a coordinate and obstructs Turing universality there. Parameterized initial syntax and variable-list folds construct named-variable substitution with an option of failure; the binder-equal branch returns the original even if the recursive result failed. Occurrence lists retain duplicates. The seven logical schemes and two rules are specified, with side conditions on substitution and quantifier distribution.

The split/quotient distinctions survive T=Th(N), which is complete yet has an associated numerical category with an inseparable pair.

## Adversarial tests to carry into comparison

* For Γ=PA an arbitrary model of PA+¬Con(PA) can accept a nonstandard alleged proof of contradiction. Test that the fully semantic arithmetic soundness claim never replaces M≡N or a standard ultrapower with all PA models, and the signed arithmetic proof uses PA-definable induction rather than external descent on nonstandard tree heights.
* For Γ=PA+¬Con(PA), a model K may disagree with N. Distinguish the model of the sentences, the standard model of the codes, and the proof-code ultrapower. Changing the standard enumeration for a c.e. axiom set need not give the same internal predicate. For Γ_e inconsistent the total q still has an output and claims no independence.
* For arbitrary X there need not be a global assignment deciding all positive and negative generators and a proper theorem/refutation join need not exclude a global element. Verify hypotheses removing these pitfalls. In absolute versus relative slice powers verify the zero-premise summand uses R₀, and uniqueness of h in the variable case is over the parameter.
