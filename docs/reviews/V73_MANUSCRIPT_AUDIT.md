## B. Corrections to the proofs

| Location in v72 | Mathematical issue | Correction in v73 |
|---|---|---|
| Corollary 3.3 | Excluding a separator proves only one part of inseparability. The two diagonal domains must also be disjoint. | The proof computes their meet by taking the inverse image of the meet of the two summand domains. |
| Theorem 5.2 | The unit of the slice adjunction and its restriction have different types. | For $i:T\hookrightarrow U$ and $q:Y\to U$, the unit is the partial inverse of $T\times_U Y\hookrightarrow Y$. Its restriction is the partial identity on that image in $Y$. |
| Lemma 6.4 | An external bound on a finite-valued tag does not itself supply a PA proof of the bound on internally finite sequences. | Reduction modulo the number of tags gives a selector whose bound PA proves. The section gives the corresponding internal finite-witness formula. |
| Theorem 9.7 | A presentation of an extension may derive the base axioms without listing them. The sentence object and the theorem domain alone do not yet identify finite provability. | Replacing axiom leaves transports finite proofs. Appendix E gives both implications between membership of the categorical theorem domain and finite Hilbert provability and points to their proof. |
| Theorem A.6 | Formula conclusions and sentence conclusions lie in different subobject orders. | Pullback along the sentence inclusion takes the closure of formulas to the generated sentence theory. The proof uses both orders to establish idempotence. |
| Proposition B.1 | A square with its pushout already in the fourth corner cannot impose a condition on $F(X)$. | The cosheaf condition is that the canonical map $F(L)\amalg_{F(D)}F(R)\to F(X)$ be bijective. |
| Proposition C.1 | Products of independent maps and pairings of two maps with the same source have different domains. | The pairing used for the restriction product has domain $D_f\cap D_g$. The copairing has domain $D_u+D_v$. |
