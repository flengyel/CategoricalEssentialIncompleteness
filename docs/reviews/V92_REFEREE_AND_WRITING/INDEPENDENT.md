# Mathematical review of Appendix E

I read the seven subsections of Appendix E at `f6c42a16479598cc739d7dcf7f64bb47af91a69a`, reconstructed the arguments from the corresponding manuscript proofs, and then compared them with the Lean declarations. The reviewer's commit `7169791` selects v90. The text of Appendix E at that commit agrees byte for byte with v89 and v91.

**E.1, restriction and extension.** Restriction composes the atomic-sentence morphism with the sentence component of the translation. Conversely, the two bounds on an assignment make its extension a theory morphism. The defining equation at atoms and uniqueness of the free interpretation give the inverse identities. Restricting or extending the indicated identity morphisms gives the unit and counit with the printed orientation. [Hom-set equivalence](../../../Lean/CategoricalEssentialIncompleteness/V46SignedFirstOrderAdjunction.lean#L27)

**E.2, separation.** Pulling back the complementary theorem and refutation subobjects of a consistent complete extension separates the given signed pair. In the other direction a separator gives a Boolean assignment. Its true fiber is closed under deduction, contains the signed generators, and is complementary to its negative pullback. This proves the equivalence under the consistency hypothesis on the generated theory. The general split-restriction-category statement and the subobject presentation are both present. [Boolean extension](../../../Lean/CategoricalEssentialIncompleteness/V46SignedBooleanExtension.lean#L28) · [General statement](../../../Lean/CategoricalEssentialIncompleteness/V46SignedFirstOrderIncompleteness.lean#L124)

**E.3, signed derivations.** In an arbitrary model of PA the single internal list assembled from both proof trees includes every positive leaf and contains only labels satisfying the positive diagonal predicate. PA-provable disjointness excludes the label of each negative leaf. Induction for the internally coded trees makes both conclusions true at that same valuation, which contradicts the negation equation. This also treats nonstandard proof codes. The model argument followed by completeness and the arithmetized induction in §8 establish the same PA conclusion. [Common support](../../../Lean/CategoricalEssentialIncompleteness/V46ArithmeticSignedContradiction.lean#L32) · [Completeness application](../../../Lean/CategoricalEssentialIncompleteness/V46ArithmeticSignedConsistencyPA.lean#L45)

**E.4, a lifted conditional.** Applying the range functor to the Turing diagonal identity and composing with the inverse coproduct comparison gives the same partial morphism by either route. Pulling back its first summand gives the pullback of the negative domain under the parameter, bounded by the pullback of the positive domain. The second summand gives the opposite bound. The two pullbacks coincide and their meet is zero. For the oracle conditional, any lift would have full domain; the output summand of such a computable total function would decide the separating set. [Comparison](../../../Lean/CategoricalEssentialIncompleteness/V46Comparison.lean#L35) · [Oracle conditional](../../../Lean/CategoricalEssentialIncompleteness/OracleMissingLift.lean#L89)

**E.5, evaluation.** Pullback along the logical theorem point takes the constant family back to its base object up to the projection isomorphism. Its naturality at the sentence inclusion identifies the evaluated closed sentence subobject with the sentence part of the transported formula subobject. Transport of the rule operations gives closure by initiality and axiom introduction. Evaluation and constant families then carry consistent complete extensions in the required two directions. [Closed theories and equivalence](../../../Lean/CategoricalEssentialIncompleteness/V46AssociatedClosedTheories.lean#L68)

**E.6, uniform sentences.** A single total parameter and the sentence map give one code assignment for all indices. A proof in a consistent fiber selects the odd summand, where PA refutes the chosen instance. A refutation selects the even summand, where PA proves it. As every fiber extends PA, both alternatives contradict consistency. This is a statement about one morphism with the indicated value at each index. [Output and sentence code](../../../Lean/CategoricalEssentialIncompleteness/V46ArithmeticUniformIndependent.lean#L116) · [Uniform theorem](../../../Lean/CategoricalEssentialIncompleteness/V46ArithmeticUniformIndependent.lean#L184)

**E.7, Miller derivations.** The unique fold to an inference algebra is a morphism over the parameter and conclusion, and satisfies the constructor equation. The finite-proof comparison identifies sentence-conclusion membership with Hilbert provability. An arbitrary axiom monomorphism is isomorphic over the sentence object to the one obtained from its image words. Relabelling along the isomorphism and its inverse gives both inclusions of conclusion images. Pullback by negation recovers the refutation domain. [Parameterized fold](../../../Lean/CategoricalEssentialIncompleteness/V46MillerParameterizedDeduction.lean#L97) · [Theorem domain](../../../Lean/CategoricalEssentialIncompleteness/V46MillerTheoryDomainApplication.lean#L59) · [Recovery of the conclusion image](../../../Lean/CategoricalEssentialIncompleteness/V46MillerCanonicalAxiomIncompleteness.lean#L172)

**Lemma A.7.** The v89 source commit `31c64416f69720e520febd3d4153eb4fce2b0403` includes both inequalities. The restriction idempotent $mr$ fixes $f$, so leastness gives $\operatorname{ran}(f)\le mr$. Factoring the image inclusion through the splitting of $\operatorname{ran}(f)$ gives the reverse inequality. Lean's proof concludes by antisymmetry. [Published correction](https://github.com/flengyel/CategoricalEssentialIncompleteness/blob/31c64416f69720e520febd3d4153eb4fce2b0403/manuscript/latex/appendices/a-syntax-and-theories.tex#L550) · [Lean proof](../../../Lean/CategoricalEssentialIncompleteness/V46RegularImage.lean#L119)
