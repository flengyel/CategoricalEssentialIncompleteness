## (C) Minor issues

1. **The morphism indexed in 2006.** Page 95, C, lines 219–222, says that the earlier proof “takes a source index for the section.” Literally this is ill-typed. The section has type $s_A:F\mathbb N\to F\mathbb N+F\mathbb N$. The weak Turing property indexes source endomorphisms $u:\mathbb N\rightharpoonup\mathbb N$. On p. 11 of the 2006 report the author identifies the functor with the identity, constructs $f$ as the section of $\nabla(\gamma^\perp+\gamma)$ and then asks for an idempotent $t$-index of $b^{-1}f$. Here $\gamma=1_A$, so the section really is $s_A$. With the categories distinguished, the missing endomorphism would satisfy $F(u)=F(b)^{-1}\chi s_A$. Such a $u$ would give the forbidden lift $\ell=bu$. The correction concerns the attribution and its types. The proposition survives it. Evidence comes from the original theorem and the types above. ## (D) Cross-section consistency failures

The two summand assignments agree across §§1 and 3, Appendix C and the 2006 source. The diagonal $b\tau_K\Delta$ sends points of $D_{a_0}$ left and points of $D_{a_1}$ right. With $P=1_A$, $N=1_{A^c}$, the conditional has inverse images $N$ on the left and $P$ on the right. Had $\ell$ existed, §3 could apply the source Turing property to $(b^{-1}\ell)\pi_2$. In this example every total $E:\mathbb N\to\mathbb N$ has $E^{-1}(A)\cup E^{-1}(A^c)=\mathbb N$, so the two inverse images cannot both be empty. The comparison theorem uses precisely the premise missing here. Proposition 3.4 supplies a source conditional from joins of morphisms with disjoint domains. Its join hypothesis fails in C.1 although the displayed **idempotent** join exists. In the manuscript dependency direction C.1 establishes a limitation on §3 and the counterexample to the old theorem. Later positive incompleteness proofs do not invoke C.1.
