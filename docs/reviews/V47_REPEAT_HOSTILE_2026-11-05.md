## (B) Major weaknesses

No major mathematical failure was established. In particular, the following possible objections do not survive inspection of the proofs:

- Proposition 5.5 proves initiality among all algebra morphisms in each parameter slice. Its proof establishes constructor commutation after unique evaluation and then performs a separate induction for uniqueness among algebra morphisms.
- Lemma 5.8 matches each child conclusion with the corresponding premise of its rule instance. The children are not constrained to have the parent's conclusion.
- Theorem 6.9 obtains its nonstandard soundness factorization by a natural comparison with an ultrapower of the standard interpretation. It does not assert an internal truth predicate for all nonstandard formula codes.
- Theorem 7.3 uses semantic consistency of the interpreted theory. PA-provability of the represented theory's consistency is not assumed.
- Theorem 7.6 obtains a total program-parameter morphism from the Turing property. It does not require the partial proof search to terminate for every input.
- Proposition 7.5 proves the required preservation of initiality using extensivity and an equivalence of algebra categories, rather than assuming that every pullback preserves an initial algebra.
- The numerical and cosheaf applications establish the universal properties and component inseparability they use. They do not infer them from preservation of regular images alone.

These are checked deductions, not corrections to the manuscript.
