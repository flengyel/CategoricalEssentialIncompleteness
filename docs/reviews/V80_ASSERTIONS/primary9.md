### Follow-up on exact-type comparisons

* **Undecided atom.** `ForkObservedIncompleteness.lean:72–94` proves inseparability of the two *atomic* idempotents and the exact atom membership equivalences. `ForkObservationFunctor.lean:90–109` transports them to tuples, proves the two c.e. predicates and rules out every computable separator. `V46Applications.lean:80–115` extracts an undecided code whose type covers *all formulas*. `V46ForkAxiomClosure.lean:157` and `V46ForkDerivationImages.lean:249` establish that same ordinary formula conclusion for arbitrary c.e. axiom sets. The missing literal proof at §10 lines 595–602 would apply atomic inseparability to the two atomic domains themselves if their union were all of ℕ²; disjointness then makes them complementary. Taking a tuple outside the union and rewriting by the two membership lemmas gives the required atom. No endpoint theorem at the revision examined was located that carries out this final extraction on atoms. This is a missing explicit consequence of checked results; ordinary endpoint incompleteness and atomic inseparability already have the printed strength.
* **Boolean equality on V.** `ForkObstruction.lean:35–54` and `EffectiveForkUniversal.lean:86–100` quantify over *all* triples of natural Bool-valued functions with pointwise equality classification. This supplies a stronger set-theoretic obstruction. For the exact printed conclusion, forget an alleged classifier in the computable diagram category, then evaluate its diagonal pullback at the three component probes; this produces the forbidden triple. A source search for these obstruction predicates and their callers found no such specialization of the pullback. `V46DiagramDecidableVariables.lean:76` instead builds the positive classifier for the constant number diagram. B.10's first conclusion has an independent complete obstruction proof.
* **Weighted cube topology.** The declaration `productDistance` and all its named uses in the repository's Lean sources are in `V46MillerCutBoxDistance.lean` (lines 150, 152, 179, 192). These establish summability and comparison with the nearest point of a box. No identified statement proves that this precise distance induces the product topology. The hand verification is short: the n-th coordinate displacement is at most 2^(n+1) times the distance; conversely restrict sufficiently many coordinates and bound the entire remaining tail by 2^(-N). The manuscript's use of continuous distance functions is checked directly by finite sums and minima, so its reduction does not depend on formalizing this comparison.
* **Sierpiński gluing.** `ForkTopology.lean:27,45–50,108–149` identifies the five opens with the actual open lattice. Their intersections with the left side are ∅, {c} and {a,c}, and similarly on the right. The sides cover X, meet in {c}, and a map out of X is continuous if its two side restrictions are continuous. These are the elementary topological subspace/gluing identifications. No separate Lean homeomorphisms or topological pushout at these named inclusions were located. B.1 proves the cosheaf equivalence from actual open-cover gluing, not by using this descriptive comparison.
