## (A) Kill-shots / hard blockers

No counterexample emerged to the characterization as stated. The equivalence concerns the **consistent theory freely generated by the pair**. For an arbitrary consistent target the result is an implication. The distinction is essential. A complementary separator of the generators gives a Boolean valuation on the free presentation and hence a complete consistent extension. An arbitrary target can have additional axioms that this valuation fails to satisfy. [Theorem 4.7](https://github.com/flengyel/CategoricalEssentialIncompleteness/blob/080fa7f0e938d41a4a560c6d9f13eeeabb1760d1/manuscript/latex/sections/03b-internal-adjunctions.tex#L382-L404) · [Corollary 4.8, both directions](https://github.com/flengyel/CategoricalEssentialIncompleteness/blob/080fa7f0e938d41a4a560c6d9f13eeeabb1760d1/manuscript/latex/sections/03b-internal-adjunctions.tex#L485-L545)

The abstract's first sentence gives the construction and the second gives this characterization. The arXiv file contains **1,917 ASCII characters**, with no terminal newline, and agrees with the paragraph in the PDF source. The earlier 1,920-character version differed at two places. Making the ordered pair singular added one character, and dropping “The” before “three examples” saved four. ## (B) Major weaknesses

**The introduction stops one implication short of the Smullyan result it advertises.** On p. 4, source lines 5–14 state Smullyan’s conclusion about inseparability of the *sets of codes*. Lines 76–81 explain that a complete consistent extension would separate the source pair. This proves essential incompleteness. It leaves the reader to find the categorical theorem about inseparability of the *target subobjects*. Corollary 4.3 proves precisely that stronger assertion. This is an expository gap in the account of the principal result. [Introduction, opening](https://github.com/flengyel/CategoricalEssentialIncompleteness/blob/080fa7f0e938d41a4a560c6d9f13eeeabb1760d1/manuscript/latex/sections/01-introduction.tex#L3-L19) · [Introduction, pullback argument](https://github.com/flengyel/CategoricalEssentialIncompleteness/blob/080fa7f0e938d41a4a560c6d9f13eeeabb1760d1/manuscript/latex/sections/01-introduction.tex#L65-L81) · [Corollary 4.3](https://github.com/flengyel/CategoricalEssentialIncompleteness/blob/080fa7f0e938d41a4a560c6d9f13eeeabb1760d1/manuscript/latex/sections/03a-internal-theories.tex#L213-L235)

The proof fits at the existing location. Let $f:X\to S$ carry $a$ into $t$ and $b$ into $\nu^*t$. For any consistent closed extension $H\ge t$, suppose complementary subobjects $C,D$ contain $H,\nu^*H$, respectively. Then $f^*C,f^*D$ are complementary and contain $a,b$. Thus an inseparable source pair makes $H,\nu^*H$ inseparable. Taking $H=t$ gives the target conclusion. Completeness of any such $H$ would make $H,\nu^*H$ themselves a separator. This supplies the forward implication. The converse for $T_{a,b}$ comes from the Boolean valuation in Corollary 4.8.

**The abstract uses “translations” for the morphisms in the adjunction.** The abstract says “translations from $T_{a,b}$ into a theory $T$.” The two signed bounds identify the intended reading. On p. 4, source lines 68–72 give the object assignments of the adjunction. Lines 76–78 give the bounds in a different argument. At their first conjunction, say that every total $f:X\to S_Q$ extends uniquely to a logical translation $P_X\to Q$. For a consistent target theory $U$, that translation is a morphism $(P_X,T_{a,b})\to(Q,U)$ precisely when $a\le f^*U$ and $b\le f^*\nu_Q^*U$. Theorem 4.7 takes pairs generating consistent theories and consistent target theories as its two object classes. [Presentations](https://github.com/flengyel/CategoricalEssentialIncompleteness/blob/080fa7f0e938d41a4a560c6d9f13eeeabb1760d1/manuscript/latex/sections/03b-internal-adjunctions.tex#L66-L79) · [The two categories](https://github.com/flengyel/CategoricalEssentialIncompleteness/blob/080fa7f0e938d41a4a560c6d9f13eeeabb1760d1/manuscript/latex/sections/03b-internal-adjunctions.tex#L358-L380) · [Hom-set correspondence](https://github.com/flengyel/CategoricalEssentialIncompleteness/blob/080fa7f0e938d41a4a560c6d9f13eeeabb1760d1/manuscript/latex/sections/03b-internal-adjunctions.tex#L459-L472)

A small test shows why the noun matters. On a presentation with one propositional atom $p$, the identity is a logical translation to that same presentation equipped with the complete Boolean theory assigning $p$ false. It fails to give a theory morphism from the theory generated by the axiom $p$. This identifies the erroneous reading of “translation” without charging the theorem with that error.

## (E) Acceptance conditions

1. On p. 4, a reader should be able to tell exactly which sentence assignments give morphisms of theories and why uniqueness already holds at the level of presentations.
2. At the same location, the reader should be able to recover the inseparability of the target theorem and refutation subobjects by pulling back an arbitrary complementary separator. Keep the converse explicitly restricted to the free theory.
3. In the arithmetic summary, identify the witness for the abstract’s existence claim and put the consistency-of-PA hypothesis at the change to $t_\pm$. In the cosheaf summary, identify the undecided atom as the conclusion of the endpoint argument.

## (F) Scope and recommendation

The strongest confirmed weakness is the missing categorical conclusion in the introduction’s own account of Smullyan. The strongest potentially misleading reading of the abstract is that an arbitrary translation of presentations transfers essential incompleteness. Its signed bounds rule out that reading when “translation” means a morphism of theories. The historical negative is supported for the announced joint treatment only.

**Recommendation for these pages:** retain the abstract’s opening, conditional comparison and three examples. Make the local corrections to §1 before incorporating the expanded universal-property statement. This review covers the abstract, §1 and the theorem passages cited above. A whole-paper acceptance judgment remains open; the v95 full Lean replay and two independent whole-paper readings are separate.
