## From the Challenge to the printed corollary

Theorem 4.7 fixes a decidable variable object $V$. For **every** index object $Y$, it assumes parameter-stable first-order syntax with nullary predicates indexed by $Y$, and initial derivation algebras for the logical rules with arbitrary nonlogical axiom subobject. The ambient total category is regular with disjoint pullback-stable finite coproducts. Every total monomorphism has a partial inverse in the restriction splitting. Theorem 4.7 uses these data to construct an adjunction. Corollary 4.8 uses its atomic sentence morphism $\theta:X\to S_X$. [Theorem 4.7 and Corollary 4.8](../manuscript/latex/sections/03b-internal-adjunctions.tex#L258)

The Challenge takes subobjects $a,b$ of $X$, just as the corollary does. Write $i_a,i_b$ for their inclusions and $\nu:S_X\to S_X$ for negation. The signed theory $T$ is generated by $\operatorname{im}(\theta i_a)\vee\operatorname{im}(\nu\theta i_b)$. The assumptions are $a\wedge b=0$ and $T\wedge\nu^*T=0$. Essential incompleteness includes this consistency condition and quantifies over **every** subobject $U$ of the same sentence object with $\operatorname{Gen}(U)=U$, $T\leq U$, and $U\wedge\nu^*U=0$. Each must satisfy $U\vee\nu^*U\ne 1$. Inseparability means disjointness and the absence of complementary subobjects $c,d$ with $a\leq c$ and $b\leq d$. [Challenge definitions and theorem](../Lean/CEIPalomar/Challenge.lean#L60)

The subobject formulation works in any regular, finitary extensive category. To recover the printed formulation take $\mathscr S=\mathrm{Split}(\mathscr R)$ and $\mathcal D=\mathrm{Tot}(\mathscr S)$. A total monomorphism $m:A\hookrightarrow X$ with partial inverse $m^\dagger:X\rightharpoonup A$ corresponds to the restriction idempotent $m m^\dagger:X\rightharpoonup X$. This order isomorphism carries disjointness, complements and both separating inequalities in both directions. Stating the idempotent formulation directly in the Challenge would require giving the restriction-category, splitting and idempotent infrastructure there. Those project modules lie outside the permitted Challenge imports. The subobject formulation uses five Mathlib imports and lets the Solution reuse the proved isomorphism. [Order isomorphism](../Lean/CategoricalEssentialIncompleteness/V46RestrictionSubobjects.lean) · [Preservation of inseparability](../Lean/CategoricalEssentialIncompleteness/V46RestrictionSubobjectInseparability.lean#L50)

For each $Y$, choose the syntax, including the decidable equality test for $V$, the parameter-stable initial term/formula algebra and the stable variable-list algebra. The arity bound is zero, the function coefficient is initial and the predicate coefficient is $Y$. For each axiom subobject $A$ choose an algebra `proofs Y A` for the rule functor `(syntaxData Y).logicalRules A.arrow` and a proof of its initiality. These are precisely the three fields of `NullaryPresentations V`. The compiled proof `isNullaryCalculus_of_data` forms `F = ⟨syntaxData, proofs, initial⟩`. At each $X$, the witness for the Solution's existential definition is `⟨F, rfl⟩`. This works for each choice of the stipulated data, before choosing a disjoint pair or assuming consistency. ## The two implications

**An inseparable pair gives essential incompleteness.** The two signed generators give $a\leq\theta^*T$ and $b\leq\theta^*\nu^*T$. Let $U$ be an arbitrary consistent closed extension. If $U$ were complete, consistency and completeness would make $U,\nu^*U$ complementary subobjects of $S_X$. Pulling them back along the total morphism $\theta$ would separate $a,b$. This contradiction gives incompleteness of each such $U$. [Atomic bounds](../Lean/CategoricalEssentialIncompleteness/V46SignedFirstOrderGeneration.lean) · [Subobject proof](../Lean/CategoricalEssentialIncompleteness/V46SignedSubobjectIncompleteness.lean#L43)

**A separator gives a complete extension.** If $c,d$ are complementary and contain $a,b$ respectively, their coproduct induces an isomorphism onto $X$. Its inverse defines an assignment $X\to 1+1$ true on $c$ and false on $d$. The right injection is true. Extend this to formulas by interpreting equality as true, negation and implication by the Boolean operations, and universal quantification by projection. Admissible substitution preserves the valuation. The logical schemes hold and the rules preserve truth. The inverse image of true on sentences is closed, consistent, complete and contains the signed generators. The compiled `complete_extension_of_separator` obtains the assignment from the separator, applies the **exported** `CEIPalomar.boolean_assignment` and returns this extension as a native closed theory. Essential incompleteness excludes it. Both implications hold for the arbitrary category and syntax above. [Separator and assignment](../Lean/CategoricalEssentialIncompleteness/V46SubobjectInseparability.lean) · [Boolean theory](../Lean/CategoricalEssentialIncompleteness/V46SignedBooleanExtension.lean) · [Compiled extension](../Lean/CEIPalomar/ManuscriptChecks.lean#L69)

## The arithmetic witness

In the PA predicate category take $V=X$ to be the number object `PrintedObjectCoding.Number` and use `ArithmeticSignedTheory.presentation`. The positive and negative subobjects are the inclusions of the two diagonal output predicates. Their underlying partial morphisms are identified with `ArithmeticDiagonalCodes.a0` and `a1`. The predicates are disjoint. A complementary partition in this category would give a computably enumerable standard set with a computably enumerable complement, hence a computable separator of the two diagonal sets. Their recursive inseparability rules this out. [Diagonal identifications](../Lean/CategoricalEssentialIncompleteness/V46ArithmeticSignedSplitGenerators.lean#L59) · [Separator argument](../Lean/CategoricalEssentialIncompleteness/V46ArithmeticSignedSeparator.lean#L145) · [Diagonal inseparability](../Lean/CategoricalEssentialIncompleteness/V46ArithmeticSignedEssential.lean#L55)

For internal consistency, collect the positive axiom labels from two finite derivations of opposite sentence conclusions. Use this same list to evaluate both derivations. Every positive leaf is true and disjointness makes every negative leaf true. The finite-support truth proof excludes the two conclusions. `pa_diagonal_witness` uses `signed_theory_consistent` and the exported equivalence on this particular pair. `exists_inseparable_example` supplies the category, its instances, the objects, calculus and pair, then applies that witness. The full manuscript lemma assumes metatheoretic consistency of PA and states inseparability in the split category, internal consistency and incompleteness of every consistent closed extension. Its separate Lean declaration is `ArithmeticSignedTheory.printed_statement`.
