## Mathematical comparisons

**Introduction and §3.** The construction of internal theories uses the regular, finitely extensive total category, the partial inverses of its monomorphisms, decidable equality for variables, and the indicated initial syntax, list and derivation algebras. In Theorem 3.1, a lift of the partial switch yields a total source morphism $g$ such that the inverse images of the two target domains $P$ and $N$ along $F(g)$ are both zero. If the interpreted identity is nonzero and inverse image along $F(g)$ preserves the specified join, then $P\vee N\ne\top$. Corollary 3.3 obtains source inseparability from a total switch for every complementary source pair, with the stated nonzero and join-preservation hypotheses. **Theory extensions in §5.** Lemma 5.1 identifies the restriction idempotent completion of the recursion category on projections $H\times X\to H$ with the partial-map category of the slice over $H$. Both comparison functors preserve restriction. For a monic theory inclusion $i$, Theorem 5.2 gives fully faithful postcomposition and the two adjunctions $\Sigma_i\dashv i^*\dashv\Sigma_i$ of the underlying categories. The constant-family result in Theorem 5.3 assumes derivation initiality for each parameter and axiom subobject and a global theorem point factoring through the consistent closed theories at issue. Its evaluation argument admits arbitrary closed consistent extensions in the slice.
