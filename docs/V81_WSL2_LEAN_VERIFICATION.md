## Proofs at the manuscript hypotheses

**Proposition 8.8, §8.4, lines 292–296.** Fix a finite arity bound and the recursive supports of the unary function and relation symbol objects at every arity through that bound. The structural induction decides the allowed labels of every postfix record. A bounded conjunction checks all records in a word. The final parser equality determines its sort. In dimension one, coding a coefficient leaves the numerical label fixed. Consequently `numerical_word_membership` gives `ComputablePred (fun w : ℕ => (Postfix.plainCarrier formula a b).val.Evalb ![w])` for either value of `formula`, where `a,b` are the actual numerical signature polynomials. The independent finite-signature check extracts two total Boolean functions with the required equivalences on **every natural number**. The readers also derived the tests for membership in the supports of the literal numerical algebra itself. **Cockett–Hofstra 4.9.** From an internal partial combinatory algebra the proof constructs the category of computable morphisms, total program parameters and all formal-power retractions. Inclusion preserves binary restriction products at every ambient source. Conversely, all morphisms between finite powers of a Turing object are computable. Splitting the idempotents on powers whose images split in the ambient category yields an equivalence with that category. Its two functors preserve restriction. The 19-root proof export includes the independent ambient-cone and restriction-preservation equations. ## Manuscript findings and changes for the Web GPT

The unrestricted Di Paola–Heller attribution in §1 omits the premise that the identity of the Turing object differs from zero. If a separator exists, the proof forces that identity to equal zero. The one-object, one-morphism category satisfies the other hypotheses and has a separator. Corollary 3.3 already states the nonzero premise. [Corrected implication](../Lean/CategoricalEssentialIncompleteness/V46DominicalLiteratureChecks.lean) · [Counterexample](../Lean/CategoricalEssentialIncompleteness/V46TrivialDominicalCategoryChecks.lean)

In §8.3, effective extensibility requires one recursive function for the fixed presentation. The function must return an independent sentence for each index of additional c.e. axioms whenever the resulting extension is consistent. In Appendix C the same letter denotes a natural-number oracle index and a partial endomorphism. The proposed patch distinguishes these types. It also corrects Di Paola–Heller’s page locator to 595 and gives an optional abstract naming the sentence morphism, the two axiom images and their deductive closure.
