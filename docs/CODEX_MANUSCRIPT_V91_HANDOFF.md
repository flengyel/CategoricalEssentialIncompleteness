Appendix D considers the family of interpretations in all models of a consistent arithmetic theory on the carrier of natural numbers. The family is faithful and its comparison square commutes strictly under theory extension. For a restriction idempotent, extension gives an inclusion between the sets of provable numeral instances. The revised correspondence table states this inclusion.

Di Paola and Montagna’s Corollary 4.4, stated on p. 657 and proved through p. 658, gives failure of local connectedness of the unprimed syntactic category for consistent c.e. extensions of PA. Corollary D.2, used for true arithmetic, requires consistency and extends to theories with no computable enumeration.
