# Manuscript v96

Section 1 distinguishes Smullyan’s representation systems from the arithmetical specialization cited by Di Paola and Heller. It states the signed bounds for a morphism of theories and obtains inseparability of the target’s theorem and refutation subobjects by pulling back a separator. It gives the consistency hypothesis for the signed arithmetic theory, the atomic conclusion at either endpoint of the cosheaf example, and the separate results of Shoenfield and Murwanashyaka–Pakhomov–Visser. Section 4 fixes the meaning of a translation of theories. [Introduction](../manuscript/latex/sections/01-introduction.tex) · [Theorem 4.7](../manuscript/latex/sections/03b-internal-adjunctions.tex)
