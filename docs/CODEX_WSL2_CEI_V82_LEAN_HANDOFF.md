## Six open historical conclusions

Retain these comparisons in the paper and its assertion inventory. None supplies a hypothesis for a numbered theorem in this manuscript. A cited result is open for whole-paper formalization until an imported or project proof establishes the conclusion at its published types. [Historical hypotheses and dependencies](reviews/V81_WSL2_ASSERTIONS/historical-comparisons.md#historical-constructions-outside-the-present-proofs) · [Mathematical constructions and equations](reviews/V81_MANUSCRIPT_RETURN/literature.md)

1. **Bauer–Hanson 6.4.** In the parameterized realizability topos $\mathcal T_\mu$ of a Miller sequence $\mu$, construct an epimorphism $q:N\to R_D$ from its natural numbers object to its object of Dedekind reals. Identify the represented interval with its internal Dedekind unit interval and obtain the epimorphism $e:N\to I_D$ with $e(n)=\mu(n)$ in that presentation. The internal formula $q(\langle m,n\rangle)=m(2e(n)-1)$ gives the extension to all Dedekind reals. Section 9 uses the external Miller witness. [Lemmas 6.1–6.3 and Theorem 6.4](https://arxiv.org/html/2404.01256v3#S6.SS1)
2. **Heller 9.2.** For a uniformly generated isotypical $B^\#$-subcategory $\mathcal B$ of a locally connected iteration category, prove that its Turing completion $\operatorname{Tur}(\mathcal B)$ is a recursion category. Construct the category of Turing developments and its Turing morphism from the specified generator and frame. The manuscript's three examples give their own Turing morphisms. [Heller, §§8–9](https://doi.org/10.2307/2274486)
3. **Lengyel 21.** For a separated iteration category $\mathcal C$ and a uniformly generated isotypical $B^\#$-subcategory $\mathcal B$ of its total morphisms, prove that $\operatorname{Tur}(\mathcal B)$ is a recursion category. The relative theorem below applied to $1_{\mathcal C}$ gives this case. The passage in §1 compares the hypotheses for constructing a recursion category. [Lengyel, §4](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0168-0072(03)00060-5)
4. **Lengyel, corrected Theorem 26.** Let $L:\mathcal C\to\mathcal D$ be a faithful iteration functor with $\mathcal D$ separated and $\mathcal B=\mathcal B\langle X\rangle$ a uniformly generated isotypical $B^\#$-subcategory of the total morphisms of $\mathcal C$. Prove that $\operatorname{Tur}(\mathcal B)$ is a recursion category. The construction reflects the finite simulations with their partial domains. In the completion obtain $\zeta:X\times X\rightharpoonup X$ such that for each $\psi:V\times X\rightharpoonup X$ there exists a total $j:V\to X$ with $\zeta(j\times1_X)=\psi$. Appendix D establishes faithfulness for its own family of interpretations directly. [Corrected theorem and universal morphism](https://github.com/flengyel/errata/blob/5555e4fa1da7b8e3a0836dd1fbf1ca385cacd03b/lengyel2004/lengyel2004_repairs_v10.tex#L619)
5. **Van Dijk–Gietelink Oldenziel 5.19.** For an arithmetic-universe object $U$ in $U_{\rm rec}$, prove that syntactic completeness of $U$ implies triviality of $U$. The authors give the diagonal construction in a special case. A proof at the published quantifier must account for each $U$ in the statement. The manuscript obtains its slice comparison in Theorem 5.3 independently. [Theorem 5.19](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2004.10482#page=25)
6. **Van Dijk–Gietelink Oldenziel 5.20.** Assume consistency of the initial arithmetic universe $U_0$. For the internal initial arithmetic universe in $U_0$, prove that the equalizer of its designated truth values $\mathrm{true}'$ and $\mathrm{false}'$ represents a subobject of $1$ distinct from the bottom subobject in $U_0$. Lemma 8.9 uses a separate derivation argument for consistency of the signed PA theory. [Theorem 5.20](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2004.10482#page=26)
