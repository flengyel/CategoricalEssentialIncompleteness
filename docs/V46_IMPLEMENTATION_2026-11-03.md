## Remaining v46 obligations

The complete target is still open. Its first concrete gaps are the arbitrary-predicate equivalence `P_T ≃ Tot(Split(S_T))` of Lemma 5.3, and the T-provably total unique evaluation graphs in every parameter slice required by Proposition 5.5. The functional-search generalization supplies a needed tool; it does not establish these universal properties.

Further obligations are the list identities and named-variable option substitution/complemented admissibility; seven logical schemes; concrete syntactic, numerical and oracle syntax/list/derivation initiality and Craig/LK comparisons; generic partial-map interpretation; semantic substitution and standard/ultrapower soundness; the partial proof-search lift and categorical Theorem 7.3 chain; indexed theories, numeral fibers and the uniform independent-sentence morphism; representation-independent Miller regularity/initiality/theory application; and cosheaf slice proof initiality, general c.e.-axiom closure and Karoubi restriction preservation.

Raw named-variable codes retain binder labels, while the existing arithmetic syntax uses de Bruijn binding. Erasing names is not injective on raw codes, so the required substitution/derivability comparison cannot be replaced by an equality of these presentations. A computed recursion or a result for one selected oracle also does not prove the stated parameter-slice or representation-independent universal property. These are outstanding constructions, not confirmed errors in the manuscript.
